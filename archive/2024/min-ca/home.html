We Win With Min

Orange County voters trust Dave Min to fight for Democratic values in Congress, because that’s what he’s been doing here at home.

Elected in 2020, California State Senator has successfully shepherded 28 bills into law, including legislation enshrining abortion in the California Constitution, expanding protections for domestic violence survivors, banning gun sales on state property, and addressing the causes of our climate crisis.

A the son of Korean immigrants and a product of California public schools, Dave is running for Congress to preserve the American Dream that brought his parents to this country, centered around the core values of diversity and opportunity. Dave has continued to champion those same values in the California State Senate, taking on MAGA extremists and working tirelessly to create an economy that uplifts Orange County’s working families.
