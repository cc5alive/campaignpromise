IN THE FLORIDA LEGISLATURE
In more than nine years in the Florida Legislature, I helped bring high-paying, high tech jobs to Central Florida in the biotech, simulation, space, software, and solar industries. And did much more...
In the Florida House, I got landmark legislation and initiatives passed to:
Protect families of fallen firefighters
Prevent dangerous street racing
Create SunRail and expand Osceola Parkway
Eliminate debt and provide principal reductions to families in foreclosure
In the Senate, I got landmark legislation and initiatives passed to:
Protect victims of sexual violence
Provide IDs for deaf Floridians
Seal records of minors with minor criminal infractions
Allow DREAMers to be admitted to the Florida Bar
Increase the high stakes testing exemption for foreign language students from one to two years.
Provide notice to tenants in foreclosure
I successfully delivered critical funding that has made a difference in the lives of many people in our community:
Billions to build I-4 Ultimate, SunRail, and Poinciana Parkway
Over $20 million to create a Poinciana Valencia Campus
$20 million to continue Kissimmee River/Northern Everglades Restoration
$1 million to restore Lake Toho
I also fought to give our students the education they deserve by pushing for smaller class sizes and a minimum teacher salary of $50,000 in order to recruit and retain the best educators.
I also fought to expand Medicaid to provide health coverage to over 800,000 more Floridians and to establish Florida’s health care exchange under the Affordable Care Act.
I have championed environmental protection and fought to preserve Florida’s greatest treasures – our springs, beaches, and precious wild places. I fought to ban fracking in Florida and protect our fragile supply of drinking water.
