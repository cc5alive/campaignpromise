“When I first ran for Congress, I promised to be an independent and effective voice for the people of Washington’s 8th Congressional District – someone willing to work with both parties to deliver real results that matter. And that’s exactly what I've done. I've worked to get bills passed that bring down the cost of prescription drugs, like insulin. I’ve taken on big oil and grocery store chains to stop corporations from price gouging. And I’ve worked with both parties on behalf of our farming communities to encourage sustainable agriculture and fix supply chain disruptions. I am so proud to fight for our district, and want to keep delivering for you.”
