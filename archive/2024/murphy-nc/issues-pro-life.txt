Pro Life
As a devout Christian and staunch believer of the unalienable right to life for all, Dr. Murphy is a proud supporter of pro-life issues. Dr. Murphy has had a lifelong allegiance to the unborn and the right-to-life.
He has been at the tip of the spear as liberals like Speaker Pelosi have tried to strike down the Hyde Amendment and other pro-life provisions. The Hyde Amendment has been included in every government funding bill since 1976, with wide bipartisan support. Dr. Murphy has cosponsored numerous other bills to protect the lives of the unborn.
