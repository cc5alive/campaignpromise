Covid
The United States has fought diligently in controlling this international pandemic, and it has found unprecedented ways of providing vaccines, healthcare, and small business relief to those affected . As a Doctor and a Member of Congress, Dr. Murphy believes a measured approach that protects compromised groups is and would have been a better approach than broad restrictions on the economy. He also worked closely with the Trump Administration in their efforts on Operation Warp Speed, which developed a COVID 19 vaccine in record time.
