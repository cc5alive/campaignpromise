Second Amendment
Congressman Murphy is a firm supporter of the Second Amendment. He fundamentally believes in the constitutional right to bear arms for law-abiding citizens so they may protect themselves and their families.
He also is an original cosponsor of the Concealed Carry Reciprocity Act, which would allow constituents to carry concealed firearms between states that allow for such methods of carrying. This right should never be infringed upon and, as a personal sportsman, Dr. Murphy will continue to fight for the Second Amendment rights for North Carolina’s Third District.
