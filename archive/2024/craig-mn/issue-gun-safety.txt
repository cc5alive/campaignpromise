Angie on the issues
Gun Safety
My Values
I’m a gun owner and supporter of the Second Amendment, but that support goes hand in hand with doing everything I can to keep our children and our communities safe from gun violence. I was proud to help pass legislation to improve gun safety nationwide, but there is more to do to ensure no American has to worry about their loved one being killed in an act of senseless violence.
What I've Done
I voted to pass the bipartisan
Safer Communities Act, the
single biggest gun safety reform in decades to keep weapons of war out of our schools, which was signed into law in 2022.
I voted for universal background checks, including guns sold online and through private sales.
