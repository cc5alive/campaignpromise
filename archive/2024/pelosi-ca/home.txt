THANK YOU
To the California Democratic Party, Young Democrats, so many Democratic Clubs, our friends in Labor and all our VIPs, our Volunteers in Politics – thank you from the bottom of my heart for your endorsements.
I’m enormously proud of your support and confidence in me as your voice in Congress. I hope that I make you proud in representing our San Francisco values as a model to the nation!
Winning gives us the opportunity to turn our values into policy to build the future that we want and to make a difference in people’s lives.
Working together in solidarity, we will uplift our communities, save our health care and preserve our freedoms and Democracy!
Our inside maneuvering in Congress can only do so much; your outside mobilization makes all our victories for freedom, justice and a more equitable future for everyone possible.
Because of you, President Biden and Democrats delivered jobs, expanded health care including veterans’ cancer care, invested in climate action now and protected our Democracy.
With your help, Democrats will take back the House, grow our majority in the Senate to bypass the filibuster and re-elect President Joe Biden and Vice President Kamala Harris!
And once we win in 2024, we will do more to invest in the Care Economy, make the Biden Child Tax Cut permanent, and codify Roe v Wade, LGBTQ Equality and an Assault Weapons Ban. We will pass the John R. Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act and The For The People Act to build a Democracy of the many and not of the money.
Because of you, we will not only be on the right side of history — we will be on the right side of the future.
Onward to a great Democratic victory!
Thank you and God bless you all!
