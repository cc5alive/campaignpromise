Meet Luz Rivas

Luz was raised in the Valley by a single mother. She earned a degree in Electrical Engineering from MIT and a Masters from Harvard.

Luz was elected to the State Assembly in 2018. She has focused on combatting climate change and improving access to math and science education.

Union nurses endorse Assemblymember Luz Maria Rivas for Congress

National Nurses United (NNU), the largest union of registered nurses in the United States, today announced they have endorsed Assemblymember Luz Maria Rivas to represent California’s 29th Congressional District, home to over 750,000 people in the San Fernando Valley of Los Angeles.

“We are proud to endorse Luz Maria Rivas in her race for Congress,” said Sandy Reding,RN, president of California Nurses Association (CNA) and vice president of NNU.
