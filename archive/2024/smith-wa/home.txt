Meet Adam
As a lifelong resident of the 9th Congressional District, it remains my greatest honor to represent the area in which I grew up and where Sara and I continue to live and raise our family.
In Congress, I am dedicated to building an inclusive economy. That means creating jobs, making our communities affordable and safe for all, and ensuring access to quality education and healthcare.
