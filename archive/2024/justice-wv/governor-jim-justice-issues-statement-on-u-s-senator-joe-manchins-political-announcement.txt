Governor Jim Justice Issues Statement on U.S. Senator Joe Manchin’s Political Announcement
November 9, 2023
Charleston, W.Va.
– Today, Governor Jim Justice issued the following statement regarding U.S. Senator Joe Manchin’s political announcement.
“Senator Joe Manchin and I have not always agreed on policy and politics, but we’re both lifelong West Virginians who love this state beyond belief, and I respect and thank him for his many years of public service,” said Governor Jim Justice.
