Accomplishments
In her first term in office Nancy has accomplished so much for the First Congressional District in her first term in office.
– Over 1,000 meetings with constituents and stakeholders in the district in her first year
– Over $100 million in grant-funded projects for the district
– Over $12 million dollars saved or returned to taxpayers in the District in her first term
– Over 20 of her bills have passed out of committee
– Had approximately 10 of her bills pass out of the House and over to the Senate
– Legislation Nancy has helped draft and pass includes the subject areas of support for law enforcement and safer communities, civil rights, military veterans issues, cyber security/national security, whistleblower protections, natural disaster-related issues, animal rights, and more
– Nancy’s quantum computing bill is being hotlined in the Senate and will be signed into law before the end of the year
– Delivered Crab Bank in Mt. Pleasant after approximately 12 weeks of negotiation on a project that stakeholders had been working on for almost 12 years
– Secured over $1 million in funding for a Cybersecurity hub in Beaufort
– Secured over $18 million for the Hilton Head Island Airport expansion
– In the process of delivering resources for multiple beach renourishment projects
– In the process of bringing over $100 million in resources back to the district for flooding and drainage projects
Not bad for her first term in office!
