A Strong Leader for a Stronger Nebraska.

As a rural Nebraska cattle rancher and a mother of three, Deb is as Nebraskan as they come. After a 35-year career in agriculture, education, and public service, Deb was elected in 2012 as the first woman to represent Nebraska for a full-term in the U.S. Senate. A prominent member of numerous committees in the Senate, Fischer has worked hard to take care of both rural and urban communities, strengthen our military, and invest in public infrastructure for all Nebraskans.
