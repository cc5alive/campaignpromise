Jason George, Business Manager
Int'1 Union of Operating Engineers, Local 49
2829 Anthony Lane S.
Minneapolis, MN 55418
Dear Jason,
The enclosed article is from the Labor World newspaper dated 9/17/24. It echoes almost exactly what a lot of us 49er's feel about your endorsement of Peter Stauber. Most of us are just too afraid of retribution to challenge you publicly.
We realize that there are Republican 49ers in the 8t District, but that doesn't mean that we should support a candidate whose goal, reflected in his voting record, is to support a former President who clearly does not hold the union's best interest. You cannot deny what our current President has done for the union.
We think it's time you come clean about why you have continually endorsed Peter Stauber. Don't tell us how Stauber has supported us in the past because that's simply not true. When you have a candidate that wouldn't support the Infrastructure Bill because of partisan politics, a bill that has brought years of work to our union, and is perhaps the most significant legislation for our union in our lifetime, cannot be viewed as a "hiccup", as you described it.
You said that if the Republicans take control in Minnesota, there is "no chance" they would turn Minnesota into a Right to Work state. This is only a belief, not backed up with facts. I believe this is a dangerous belief when you are responsible for hundreds of 49ers and their families counting on a good-living wage.
Why don't you write a letter to Labor World and explain to the 49ers why you feel the need to support a union busting candidate, when you have a candidate in Jen Schultz, who is a strong union supporter. Your last excuse was that you didn't know about her earlier doesn't fly this time. We'll be anxious to hear your reason, but more importantly, we would like to hear the truth as to why you support Stauber.
Respectfully,
49ers for Jen Schultz
cc. Eugene Grover, President
