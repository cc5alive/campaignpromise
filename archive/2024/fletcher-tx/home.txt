In 2018, Lizzie stood up for our community and our values, becoming the first Democrat in more than 50 years and first woman ever to represent Texas’ Seventh District. In 2024, we need to stand together again.
LIZZIE PANNILL FLETCHER
DEMOCRAT FOR CONGRESS
TEXAS’ 7TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT
No place is more welcoming, diverse, innovative, hard-working, or generous than Houston. As our representative to the U.S. Congress, I am proud to represent that Houston: a city that opens its doors to newcomers from around the world, prides itself on scientific discovery, serves as a hub for innovation, works together, and takes care of its neighbors.
I am committed to working to ensure that our community’s core values—inclusion, innovation, and collaboration—result in progress, equality, and opportunity for all Americans.
