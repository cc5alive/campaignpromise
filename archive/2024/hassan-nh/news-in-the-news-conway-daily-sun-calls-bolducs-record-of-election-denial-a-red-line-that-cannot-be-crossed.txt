Conway Daily Sun Calls Bolduc’s Record of Election Denial a “Red Line that Cannot Be Crossed”
Friday, November 4, 2022
The Conway Daily Sun — which endorsed Kelly Ayotte in 2016 — yesterday published an editorial saying clearly that “democracy starts at home, and in New Hampshire that means stopping election-deniers Republican congressional candidate Karoline Leavitt and senatorial candidate Don Bolduc.”
The editorial board this cycle endorsed other Republican candidates up and down the ballot — but said that Bolduc’s record of supporting the overturning of election results “is a red line that cannot be crossed and transcends policy differences between the two parties.”
Read the editorial from the Conway Daily Sun:
Conway Daily Sun: The Sun’s endorsements
Democracy starts at home, and in New Hampshire that means stopping election-deniers Republican congressional candidate Karoline Leavitt and senatorial candidate Don Bolduc.
Is democracy really in danger? Consider the words of prominent Republican national pollster Frank Luntz. “This country is on the precipice,” he said. “What Trump (and his followers) are doing is destructive to democracy.”
This is a red line that cannot be crossed and transcends policy differences between the two parties. On Jan. 6, 2021, 147 members of Congress followed then-president Donald Trump’s directive and voted against certifying the 2020 election results. If given the opportunity, you can count on Leavitt and Bolduc to join those lawmakers. It is a chance we cannot [afford] to take […]
