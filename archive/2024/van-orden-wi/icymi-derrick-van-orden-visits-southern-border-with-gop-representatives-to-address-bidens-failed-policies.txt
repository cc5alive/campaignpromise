PRAIRIE DU CHIEN, WI – On Monday, Derrick Van Orden joined Representatives Tony Gonzalez and Markwayne Mullin at the southern border in Texas to discuss President Biden’s policy failures there and how they have contributed to the growing Fentanyl crisis gripping the nation and Wisconsin’s 3rd District. This comes after local law enforcement outlined the impact of drug smuggling in the 3rd District, and the growth in Fentanyl overdose deaths seen across the region
Derrick conducted several interviews speaking about the dangers of lax border policies and how they contribute to this growing trend in overdose deaths. Check them out below:
