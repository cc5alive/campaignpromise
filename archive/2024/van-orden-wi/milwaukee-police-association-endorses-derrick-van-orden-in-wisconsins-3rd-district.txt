Milwaukee Police Association Endorses Derrick Van Orden in Wisconsin’s 3rd District
by
Justin Giorgio
|
Jul 12, 2022
Today, the Milwaukee Police Association announced they are endorsing Derrick Van Orden for Congress in Wisconsin’s 3rd District. Law Enforcement from across the state recognize that we need leaders in Washington who will work to help them fight the rising tide of crime.
Derrick Van Orden released the following statement accompanying the endorsement:
“Across Wisconsin, Biden and Pelosi’s open borders and anti-police agenda have emboldened criminals, led to a flow of drugs that are killing Americans at an unprecedented rate, and created a rift in trust between law enforcement and our communities. I am deeply grateful for the support of the Milwaukee Police Association, as they know I proudly Back the Blue and know that with real Leadership in Washington, we can keep our communities safe again.”
