Join Michael Baumgartner for free pizza, beverages and a discussion of issues that are important to veterans. Come to David's Pizza, 803 W. Mallon Avenue in Spokane, on Wednesday, May 1, at 17:30 (5:30 p.m.). This event is free and open to anyone.

About the event
This is a free event to share pizza, beverages and your thoughts with Eastern Washington’s next 5th Congressional District representative. It is hosted by Dan Bisbee, former CPT, US Army; Andrew Rolwes, retired LTC, US Army; Chris Ferrez, retired LTC, US Air Force; Hillary Pham, former CPT, US Air Force; Dave Lucas, retired LTC, US Marine Corps; and Erich Ebel, former SGT, US Army.
