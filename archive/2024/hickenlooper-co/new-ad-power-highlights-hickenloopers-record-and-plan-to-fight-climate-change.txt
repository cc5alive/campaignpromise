New Ad “Power” Highlights Hickenlooper’s Record and Plan to Fight Climate Change
Today, Hickenlooper for Colorado released a new ad, “
Power
,” which highlights John Hickenlooper’s record of combating climate change by replacing fossil fuels with renewable energy. In the Senate, John will expand on that work to protect our planet and create jobs of the future.
Ad Transcript:
Solar power, wind power… This is how we fight climate change.
When I was Governor, we got coal plants shut down and replaced with clean energy.
In Washington, they’re ignoring climate change and still investing in the energy of the past.
Let’s get rid of the $43 billion in tax breaks for big oil and focus on clean energy instead.
That’s how we protect our planet and create jobs in the future.
I’m John Hickenlooper. I approve this message. Washington’s got to get its priorities straight.
