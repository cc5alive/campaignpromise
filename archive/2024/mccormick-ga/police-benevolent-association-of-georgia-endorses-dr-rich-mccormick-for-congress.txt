Police Benevolent Association of Georgia Endorses Dr. Rich McCormick for Congress
The Police Benevolent Association of Georgia, which is “The Voice of Law Enforcement Officers” has endorsed Marine Pilot and Emergency Room Doctor Rich McCormick in Georgia’s 6th Congressional District. The organization “will ask our members, their friends and families, and all citizens who respect the strong and efficient enforcement of our laws to cast their ballots in the upcoming election” for the Republican nominee.
“I am proud to be endorsed by an organization that represents some of the bravest men and women in our community and state,” said Dr. McCormick. “As radical politicians push to Defund The Police and advance policies that spur lawlessness in our streets, I remain steadfast in my support of law enforcement and a tough on crime agenda that keeps Georgians safe.
“Now, more than ever, we must Back The Blue and stand shoulder to shoulder against the woke mob and efforts to undermine public safety. I look forward to doing my part in Congress to support those in uniform and safeguard our future.”
