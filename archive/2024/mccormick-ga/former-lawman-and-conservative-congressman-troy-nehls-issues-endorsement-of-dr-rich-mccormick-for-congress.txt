Former Lawman and Conservative Congressman Troy Nehls Issues Endorsement of Dr. Rich McCormick for Congress
Law and Order Congressman Applauds McCormick’s “unwavering support of law enforcement”
Today, longtime lawman and Army veteran, Congressman Troy Nehls (TX-22), endorsed Dr. Rich McCormick, citing his strong support for law enforcement. Nehls, who served as Sheriff of Fort Bend County, is an unapologetic defender of President Trump and an outspoken advocate for law-and-order policies that keep Americans safe.
“I spent 30 years in law enforcement, including 8 years as Sheriff Fort Bend County, Texas,” said Congressman Nehls. “Dr. McCormick’s unwavering support for law enforcement is essential to keeping our communities safe. I need Dr. McCormick fighting with me in Washington D.C. to end the dangerous defund the police ideology being pushed by the radical left. Please join me in supporting Rich McCormick for Congress.”
“The radical left wants to defund the police, confiscate our guns, and make us surrender the rights and freedoms guaranteed in the United States Constitution,” said Dr. McCormick. “I am running for Congress so I can stand side-by-side with Troy Nehls to stop them!
