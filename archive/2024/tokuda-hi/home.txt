FOR YOU, HAWAIʻI
I am motivated every single day to work hard, to be a true public servant for our district, and to fight for our families. Together, we’ve forged a strong foundation, but there’s so much work to be done.
My dedication to addressing critical issues is unwavering – from tackling gun violence in our communities to guaranteeing equitable healthcare and mental healthcare access. These are not just policy goals; they are moral imperatives, deeply ingrained in who we are as a community.
I intend to continue what I’ve always done – engaging with our communities directly. From Hilo to Hanalei and everywhere in between, each conversation, each shared story, enriches our collective mission. Your aspirations, your challenges, your dreams – they are what drives me every day.
