Meet Catherine
Born and raised in Las Vegas, Catherine Cortez Masto has dedicated her life to fighting for and protecting Nevadans. After serving two terms as Nevada’s Attorney General and chief law enforcement officer, Catherine made history as the first woman from Nevada and the first Latina ever elected to the Senate. Catherine is delivering for Nevada families. She’s working with Democrats, Republicans, and Independents to get things done -- always putting Nevada first.
