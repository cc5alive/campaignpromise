Ashley Hinson launches new television ad: “Debate”
Marion, IA –
Ashley Hinson, the Republican nominee for Congress in Iowa’s First District, is releasing a new television ad titled “Debate.” The ad will air across the district. Congresswoman Finkenauer has declined additional debate offers with Ashley Hinson because her schedule is “too busy.” Cleary, Congresswoman Finkenauer is too afraid to defend her liberal record in front of voters in Iowa’s First District. Hinson has accepted debate offers from KWWL, Iowa’s News Now, KCRG-TV9 and The Gazette, and WHO13.
“Iowans deserve a leader who won’t take them for granted. I will never be too busy for Iowans.”
– Ashley Hinson
Ashley is in the highest tier of the ‘Young Gun Program’ by the National Republican Congressional Committee after establishing her clear path to victory. National political forecasters believe the 1st Congressional District will be one of the most competitive races in 2020.
Ashley has a record of winning tight elections. She won in 2018 in a swing district to help hold the Iowa House majority and outperformed the Republican ticket by 5-8 points across House District 67, the heart of IA-01. She won 9 of 13 precincts in House District 67 despite Democratic outside groups outspending Republican outside groups.
