Ashley Hinson launches first television ad: Watch It

Marion, IA – Ashley Hinson, the Republican nominee for Congress in Iowa’s First District, is releasing her first TV ad titled “Watch It.” The ad will air across the district. Hinson is running to end the chaos and dysfunction in Washington on both sides of the aisle. A proven leader and respected legislator, Hinson is ready to give Congress a dose of Iowa common sense.

“For years I reported the news. Today, I can’t even let my kids watch it. Washington politicians yelling, attacks on police, socialists tearing down anyone and anything they disagree with. America has challenges, but we are still the greatest country in the world, and it’s not even close. America is worth fighting for.” – Ashley Hinson

Ashley is in the highest tier of the ‘Young Gun Program’ by the National Republican Congressional Committee after establishing her clear path to victory. National political forecasters believe the 1st Congressional District will be one of the most competitive races in 2020. The Cook Political Report has rated IA-01 as “Toss Up.”

Ashley has a record of winning tight elections. She won in 2018 in a swing district to help hold the Iowa House majority and outperformed the Republican ticket by 5-8 points across House District 67, the heart of IA-01. She won 9 of 13 precincts in House District 67 despite Democratic outside groups outspending Republican outside groups.
