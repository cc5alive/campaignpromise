About the Congresswoman
Congresswoman Norma Torres represents California’s 35th Congressional District, which includes the cities of Chino, Chino Hills, Eastvale, Fontana, Montclair, Ontario, Pomona, Rancho Cucamonga, and Upland. As a City Councilmember, Mayor, State Assemblywoman, State Senator, and now Member of Congress, she has a proven record of delivering solutions for Inland Empire families.
