Meet Donald

Donald Norcross is leading the charge to raise wages and grow our economy for America’s workers. As a graduate of community college and a skilled apprenticeship program, he understands the need for more accessible, affordable higher education options to train students for rewarding careers. As Donald fights for jobs and opportunity for all Americans, he knows that security is job one, and he is committed to supporting the brave men and women that protect our nation and our neighborhoods.
