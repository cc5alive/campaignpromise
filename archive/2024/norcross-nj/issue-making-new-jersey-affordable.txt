Making New Jersey Affordable
In a turbulent economy, I will continue to fight to keep New Jersey affordable for families and businesses to thrive. By continuing my efforts to bring jobs to the area, maintain and build infrastructure, and bring funding to jobs training programs, together we can keep our community affordable.
