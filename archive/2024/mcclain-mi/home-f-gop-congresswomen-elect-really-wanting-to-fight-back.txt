Michigan's Continued COVID Restrictions
Enough is Enough
MACOMB COUNTY, MI – Congresswoman-elect Lisa McClain (MI-10) says “Enough is Enough” in response to the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services announcement yesterday, delivered by Governor Whitmer, imposing continued restrictions on Michigan’s citizens and visitors leading well into December.
“This started out in March as two weeks to flatten the curve, and now we are in December looking at restrictions carrying on through the rest of the year and, let’s face it, likely into next year” stated McClain. “This is crippling small businesses in Michigan’s 10th District and throughout the state. Small businesses are closing for good, laying off staff and causing financial hardship on hardworking men and women.”
McClain noted that quarantines are for the sick and not the healthy. While we must be mindful of the coronavirus and respect it, we cannot live in fear of it and be paralyzed by the potential of catching it. Many restaurants and bars count on holiday parties and events to build profits to operate year-round, and only offering take out doesn’t cut it. There is a better way forward.
“Governor Whitmer has refused to work with the Legislature on common sense solutions where business owners and the people of Michigan can stay open in a responsible manner, as many other states are. Her and her department’s lack of collaboration, and even meaningful discussions, circumvent the structure of Government and how it is meant to function,” stated McClain.
McClain believes that the Governor’s unilateral actions are not in line with federal and state Constitutions and fly in the face of the recent Michigan Supreme Court’s decision, clarifying that she must work with the Legislature.
Lisa is a conservative outsider with a business background. She has never held public office before and looks forward to serving the citizens of the 10th Congressional District as she steps into the public service arena.

The Death Tax Repeal Pledge
I am pleased to announce that I signed the Death Tax (Estate Tax) Repeal Pledge. As a fiscal conservative, I will fight to repeal this tax, and countless others, that unfairly take more money out of your pocket to grow government and stifle private sector growth.
