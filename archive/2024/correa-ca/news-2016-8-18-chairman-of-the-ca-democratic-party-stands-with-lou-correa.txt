Endorsement from Chairman of the California Democratic Party John Burton Demonstrates Correa’s Widespread Appeal
Santa Ana, CA
—Following an outpouring of support from grassroots activists and local leaders as well as a recent groundbreaking endorsement from the California Democratic Party, today, former State Senator Lou Correa won the support of John Burton, Chairman of the California Democratic Party, in the race to represent California’s 46th Congressional District.
In announcing his support for Correa,
Chairman of the CA Democratic Party John Burton
released the following statement:
“I served with Lou Correa in the State Legislature, where his hard work and dedication to his district earned my respect. Today, he has my full support and endorsement. Lou will make an outstanding Congressman and represent the people of Orange County and all of California with the highest level of public service.”
Upon hearing the news,
former State Senator Lou Correa
commented:
“It is an absolute honor to have the official support of CDP Chairman John Burton. As one of the most forward-thinking and inspirational leaders in California, his support means a great deal to me. I look forward to building coalitions in Congress and working alongside my fellow legislators to address the issues that working families care about. In Washington, I will fight to expand economic opportunities for workers, institute comprehensive immigration reform, improve our public education system, and increase public safety.”
