Serving Orange County In Congress
Congressman Lou Correa is proud to represent California's 46th District. As the son of working-class parents, Lou learned first hand about hard work, gratitude, and dedication. Since 1998, Lou has worked tirelessly to help families throughout Orange County live better lives and have a shot at the middle class.
Lou has a track record of getting the job done and has developed a reputation with his constituent as a dedicated civil servant interested in solving real problems, not playing partisan games.
In the past, Lou has represented the 69th Assembly District in the California State Assembly, served as Supervisor of Orange County’s 1st Supervisor District, and represented the 34th Senate District in the California State Senate.
