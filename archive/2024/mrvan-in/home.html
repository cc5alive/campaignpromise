Frank on the Issues

  • Growing Northwest Indiana’s economy

    While in Congress, Frank has secured millions of dollars in federal investments that have grown the region’s economy.

  • Protecting Women’s Reproductive Freedoms

    Freedom is on the ballot this year. Frank is committed to protecting Women’s access to reproductive health care and supports the codification of Roe.

  • Fighting for Working Men and Women

    Frank is in fighting for working men and women by by lowering costs and bringing jobs to Northwest Indiana.

  • Safe, Secure and Humane Border Security

    This nation’s border is a matter of national security and Frank believes in securing the border with safe, secure and humane policies.
