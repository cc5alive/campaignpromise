Standing Up for America’s Seniors
+ Share
Social Security and Medicare are two of the most successful programs in our nation’s history, moving – and keeping – millions of people out of poverty and enabling them to live their golden years with dignity. Nevertheless, they have been under constant attack by Republican members of Congress as well as former President Trump.
Raja believes that Social Security and Medicare are sacred promises that our country made to all American citizens. He has fought in Congress to protect those programs, to secure necessary funding for them, and to ensure they exist for future generations. Now is not the time to shirk our commitment to America’s seniors, but rather to strengthen these programs and make sure they remain strong for our children and grandchildren.
