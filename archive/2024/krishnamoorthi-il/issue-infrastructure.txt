Improving America’s Infrastructure
+ Share
Our infrastructure is the foundation of our economic success, and Congressman Raja is committed to leveraging federal investments for the benefit of working families throughout Illinois by repairing and restoring our roads, bridges, waterways, and transportation systems. In 2021, Congress passed and President Biden signed the historic Bipartisan Infrastructure Law. This legislation is a bold, long-term investment in our nation’s infrastructure that creates more jobs, drives long-term economic growth, and ensures a cleaner, safer, and more accessible transportation network for the benefit of all.
The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law included a number of provisions that are essential for Illinois 8th District, including robust federal funding for public transit systems, as well as safer bike and pedestrian infrastructure. This legislation included nearly $15 million for the expansion of electric vehicle charging infrastructure in Illinois, as well as $94 million for improvements to Chicago Union Station and $117 for Metra commuter improvements on lines in the northwest suburbs.
Locally, Raja works closely with state and local officials to monitor progress on one of Illinois’ most important infrastructure projects, the completion of the Elgin-O’Hare Western Access project, primarily located west of O’Hare in the 8th district. Expected to be completed in late 2025, this $4 billion project will significantly reduce travel time to O’Hare International Airport, address congestion issues on local roads, attract business investment in the communities west of O’Hare and create as many as 65,000 good-paying jobs to our region by 2040.
