41st Congressional District

Ken Calvert is a lifelong resident of Corona, which is the largest city in the newly drawn 41st Congressional District. Other cities in the district include Norco, Lake Elsinore, Wildomar, Canyon Lake, Menifee, Calimesa, Palm Springs, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, as well as portions of Eastvale, and Riverside.
