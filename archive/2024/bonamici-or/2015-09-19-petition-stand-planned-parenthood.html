Petition: Stand with Planned Parenthood

Throughout the month of September, anti-choice extremists in Congress have been busy spinning deceptive stories about Planned Parenthood. They held a hearing titled “Planned Parenthood Exposed: Examining the Horrific Abortion Practices at the Nation's Largest Abortion Provider," and they didn’t even invite anyone from Planned Parenthood to testify!

It’s clear that the purpose of these hearings is to mislead the public about a vital provider of health care and family planning for women and men across the U.S.

I won’t stand for these baseless attacks, and I hope you won’t either.

Sign the Petition: tell Congress we’ve had enough of the attacks on Planned Parenthood!

These hearings have nothing to do with exposing the truth. One member of Congress even said “I don’t need an investigation to know what’s going on here.” Naturally, he and his cohorts don’t care about evidence when their goal is to take down Planned Parenthood at all costs – some are even threatening to shutdown the government over this issue.

This relentless war on Planned Parenthood is an attack on the rights of women to make their own reproductive health care decisions.

I’ve heard from countless Oregonians that these attacks are a waste of time and resources, and they need to end. It’s time to stand up for women’s health and our reproductive rights once again, and tell Congress to focus on what really matters.

Please join the call. Add your name. And let’s stand with Planned Parenthood!

With appreciation,

Posted on September 19, 2015.
