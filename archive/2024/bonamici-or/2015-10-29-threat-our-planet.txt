A threat to our planet
Climate change is a serious threat to our country and our planet, and addressing it should be at the very top of our policy agenda. But did you notice that it was barely mentioned in last night’s Republican debate?
It’s time to listen to the scientists, set aside differences, and work on solutions.
Donate now and together we can work to make climate change a top priority.
As a member of the House Committee on Science, Space, and Technology, I have seen some of our nation’s brightest scientists attacked for speaking the truth. Rather than criticizing scientists for doing their jobs, we should be listening to them and addressing the problem at the source: carbon pollution.
I will keep defending science, standing up to polluters and climate change deniers, and working toward real solutions to the climate crisis.
Please join me.
Donate now for a Congress that finds progressive solutions our nation’s needs.

Meet Suzanne
Suzanne knows what it’s like to struggle to make ends meet. She worked her way through community college, university, and law school. She started her career at Legal Aid and worked as a consumer rights attorney. Throughout her career she’s been a leading advocate for public education, protecting the environment, and civil rights. She’s fighting for a better future for all Oregonians.
