Add your name: Stand up to anti-choice "clinics"
Women need and deserve comprehensive health care treatment options from qualified professionals, and nothing less. This is especially important when faced with an unplanned pregnancy.
Unfortunately, too many women don’t get the care they need and deserve. Across the United States, thousands of so-called “Crisis Pregnancy Centers” use deceptive advertising to mislead women into thinking they are going to get comprehensive care, including the choice of a legal abortion, when in fact they get lectures, misinformation, and ideological judgment.
It’s time we stand up to the egregious practices of these anti-choice “clinics."
Sign the Petition: tell Congress to ban deceptive advertising by “Crisis Pregnancy Centers!”
