Conservative Fighter Rep. Brett Guthrie Files for Re-Election

Starts Campaign with $2 Million Cash on Hand

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (Nov. 30, 2023) – U.S. Representative Brett Guthrie (R-KY) filed for re-election today to continue serving Kentucky’s Second Congressional District in the U.S. House of Representatives. Throughout his time in office, Guthrie has stood strong for Kentuckians’ conservative values and led the fight against Joe Biden’s radical agenda.

“I hear from Kentuckians every day that life has become unaffordable. We are living with this crushing economic anxiety and the knowledge that Joe Biden doesn’t care what his policies have done to you and your family,” said Congressman Guthrie. “To stop Biden, my answer is simple: we must win. And when we do, a strong Republican majority in Congress will put our nation back on track.”

“I’m running for re-election to stop Biden and to turn the world right-side up,” Guthrie continued. “We don’t have to accept a government that mocks our hometown values and turns a blind eye to the hardships facing working people. We can turn things around, but we have to win to do it.

Guthrie represents Bowling Green—Kentucky’s third largest city—as well as Abraham Lincoln’s birthplace, Mammoth Cave, and Fort Knox. He is the Chair of the Health Subcommittee on the House Energy and Commerce Committee, where he leads the fight to lower healthcare and prescription drug costs and protect Social Security and Medicare. He’s also fighting to stop out-of-control spending, to secure the Southern Border, and to uphold the fundamental principles of our Constitution.

“It is the honor of a lifetime to fight for Kentucky in Congress,” said Guthrie. “I’m more energized than ever to put our nation back on track and make the American Dream possible for generations to come.

Guthrie’s campaign committee ended the last quarter with more than $2 million cash on hand.
