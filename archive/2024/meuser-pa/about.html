About Dan Meuser

Congressman Dan Meuser is a proven job creator and a servant leader for Pennsylvania, who has stood by President Trump to make America great again.  Dan ran for Congress because he believes our politics are broken, and we need return our nation to the values that make us great.  But unfortunately, rather than rallying around America, too many career politicians on the left are more interested in scoring cheap political points.   Dan believes that now, more than ever, we need leaders who will step up and make our government work for the people again.

 

Growing Up Middle Class

Dan was raised by a hardworking family – a police detective father and his stay-at-home mom who raised him and his siblings his brother and sister before rejoining the workforce.  It was from his parents that Dan learned the dignity of hard work, the value of public service, and the duty to help others.  He also learned that a problem was just an opportunity to make things better.

After high school, Dan attended the New York Maritime University and transferred to Cornell University on a Navy ROTC scholarship.

 

Creating Jobs

Dan would eventually join a small company his family founded called Pride Health Care (later named Pride Mobility Products). Dan helped establish Pride into an industry leader in the power mobility field, building Pride into a large employer that has been named one of Pennsylvania’s best places to work. At Pride Mobility, Dan would serve as the president for Pride USA for 7 years, and helped create thousands of family-sustaining jobs, while improving the quality of life for people around the World.

 

Creating Jobs

In 2011, Governor Tom Corbett nominated Dan to serve as Pennsylvania’s Secretary of Revenue, where Dan worked to reform the Department of Revenue, increase efficiencies, and put into place  customer-first procedure systems that put Pennsylvanians first.   Dan reminded the Department’s employees every day that their decisions should be aligned with putting more money in the pockets of taxpayers, transforming the Department from a tax collection agency to a taxpayer advocacy agency.  Under Dan’s leadership, the Department of Revenue was recognized by the Council on State Taxation for having the most dramatic improvement of any department of revenue in the county.

 

Ready to Serve

Dan has put his experience and commitment to service to work for the people of Northeast Pennsylvania in Congress.  Now serving in his third term in the House of Representatives, Dan has become a go-to conservative problem solver who can be counted on to always put America First.  As a member of the House Financial Services Committee and chairman of the Small Business Committee’s subcommittee on Economic Growth, Tax, and Capital Access, Dan is fighting each and every day for Northeast Pennsylvania’s small businesses that are struggling under the weight of  Bidenomics.  Dan sees first-hand the damage the Biden Administration has caused to everyday , and Americans and he is leading the effort in Pennsylvania to return President Trump to the White House.

 

Investing in Our Community

Dan is most proud of the work he has done in the communities across the district. Dan and his wife of 34 years, Shelley, have raised their three children in our community the heart of Northeast Pennsylvania.  It is It is here that Dan supported countless charities, neighborhood improvement efforts, and worked to elect strong, conservative leaders to lead Northeast Pennsylvania forward.
