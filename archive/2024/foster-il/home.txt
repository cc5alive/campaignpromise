Meet Bill
Bill is running for reelection in Illinois’ new 11th Congressional District. The district includes parts of two of Illinois’ largest cities, Aurora and Naperville. The new IL-11 includes parts of all five of Chicago’s collar counties, plus sections of Boone, Cook, and Dekalb counties.
