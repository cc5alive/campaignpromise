Dedicated to Transparency for
“We The People”

Cory believes that the American people are the greatest “checks and balances” against government abuses of power. Our beloved Constitution begins with three words, “We the People.” Our elected officials in Congress are elected by their constituencies to be “Representatives” of the people. That is precisely the role in which Cory intends to serve.

It’s time to re-empower the voice of “We The People” in Florida’s 7th District. Too much partisanship and radical division has weakened our great nation. Paraphrasing President Abraham Lincoln, Cory intends to be a voice of the people, by the people, and for the people.
