CONGRESSMAN VAN DREW ISSUES STATEMENT ON PRESIDENT BIDEN FORCING AMERICAN WORKERS TO GET VACCINATED
November 5, 2021
Congressman Van Drew issued the following statement on President Biden’s vaccine mandates that threaten American businesses and employees.
“President Biden and his administration are single-handedly ruining the greatest country to ever exist,” said Congressman Van Drew. “We are struggling to sustain an available workforce and these authoritarian vaccine mandates on vital industries will only exacerbate the labor shortage we currently face. We are already witnessing walkouts from our police officers, firefighters, and first responders. Now, because of these vaccine mandates, we are hearing the same threats from the trucking and rail industries. Our economy will never get back on track if this administration continues to exert government overreach on our American businesses. This mandate will strip millions of workers from the right to make their own personal health decisions. It is completely unacceptable, and to put it simply, it is un-American.”
