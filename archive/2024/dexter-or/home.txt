Meet Maxine
Each day, the spread of right-wing extremism and disinformation undermines our rights and democracy. Our next congressperson must be prepared on day one. As a physician and state representative, I always listen to the challenges Oregonians face, look at the data and science, and work with others to find solutions.
In the state legislature, I found common ground to successfully:
secure historic housing investments, improve public safety, expand abortion rights, protect the environment, increase addiction treatment, and close firearm law loopholes.
But when I help patients struggling to afford care they need, or work to help patients find housing, I see these victories aren't enough. We need a progressive who will get results in Congress.
I will use my experience as a physician and representative to:
Address our homelessness and addiction crises:
Build more shelters and housing, increase federal support for addiction and mental health services.
Ensure healthy communities:
Champion universal healthcare, including abortion, hold Big Pharma and Big Oil accountable, and expand renewable energy.
Rebuild a fair economy with opportunity for all:
Fight for high-quality schools, universal childcare, good-paying union jobs, and a lower cost of living.
