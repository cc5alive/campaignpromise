Meet Marie
In Congress
MARIE IS RANKED AS ONE OF THE MOST BIPARTISAN LAWMAKERS IN THE CAPITOL.​
Marie is focused on what working people want, like good jobs that don’t require a college degree, lower costs for medication and childcare, and safer communities.

right to repair
Marie knows firsthand how important it is to be able to repair your own equipment. She is working to stop manufacturers from gouging customers.
Reproductive Choice
Marie is fighting to ensure every woman can see a doctor whenever she wants, for whatever reason. She will do whatever it takes to prevent a national abortion ban.​
working families
Marie is working to bring down the cost of housing, increase choices for childcare options, and cut out the middlemen who jack up the cost of healthcare.
Secure Communities
Marie will always fight to make sure state and local law enforcement agencies have the resources they need to keep drugs and illegal guns off of our streets. ​​
