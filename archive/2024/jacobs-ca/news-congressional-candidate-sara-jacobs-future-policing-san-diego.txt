The San Diego Union-Tribune
But as difficult and heavy as everything is right now, I have hope. I have seen people who have spoken up and spoken out for the first time. Community members from all backgrounds and beliefs coming together and demanding change. Young people who are realizing how powerful their voices can be.
When we look throughout history — whether it was the civil rights movement, the LGBTQ+ rights movement, gun violence prevention, climate action or what we are seeing right now on racial justice — change has really happened when young people have gotten involved. We must, once and for all, say that Black Lives Matter. And we must empower the next generation of leaders to build the future we all deserve.
