SAN DIEGO (KUSI) – 53rd Congressional District candidate Sara Jacobs joined Good Morning San Diego to her race and COVID-19 response, specifically her experience at UNICEF, where they worked on vaccine distribution.
Jacobs discussed how the government count implement vaccine distribution once a vaccine is secured and her plan to help small businesses recover from COVID19 pandemic recession if she were to be elected to congress.
Jacobs is going against San Diego councilmember Georgette Gomez.
