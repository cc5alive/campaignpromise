Meet Chris Deluzio
Congressman Chris Deluzio is a Thornburg native, Iraq War veteran, and voting rights attorney. After graduating from the U.S. Naval Academy, Chris served as an officer in the Navy and deployed overseas multiple times. As a lawyer, Chris worked to protect voting rights and our elections as the policy director at Pitt Cyber and previously at the Brennan Center for Justice.

Core Values
Defending Democracy
Chris is deeply committed to defending our democracy and freedom to vote, and believes that we need bold action to protect and strengthen American democracy.
Reproductive Freedom
Chris believes in the simple idea that women should have the freedom to make their own healthcare and reproductive decisions: a basic human right.
The Union Way of Life
Chris stands with the labor movement and supports the union way of life. At Pitt he fought for a union with the Steelworkers, and in Congress he will always fight to protect hard-earned pensions and outsourcing of jobs.
