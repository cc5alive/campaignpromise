Chris believes in the simple idea that women should have the freedom to make their own healthcare and reproductive decisions: a basic human right. With the Supreme Court overturning
Roe v. Wade
, we cannot allow right-wing extremists and the government to interfere with a woman’s decision regarding their health. That means we need federal and state action to codify the right to an abortion and a woman’s right to decide what’s best for them. Chris will fight hard in Washington to pass the
Women’s Health Protection Act
(which would protect abortion rights under federal law) and to oppose far-right efforts in the states to eliminate abortion rights. Furthermore, as someone who believes that every American should have the guarantee of good healthcare, Chris will work to make sure that healthcare includes full and meaningful reproductive coverage.
