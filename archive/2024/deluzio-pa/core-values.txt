Core Values
Defending Democracy
Chris is deeply committed to defending our democracy and freedom to vote, and believes that we need bold action to protect and strengthen American democracy.
Make Stuff Here
Chris believes that we should be making the products Americans depend on here in America, and that we should be making them with union workers right here in Western PA.
Reproductive Freedom
Chris believes in the simple idea that women should have the freedom to make their own healthcare and reproductive decisions: a basic human right.
Securing Your Retirement
Chris believes that every American should be able to retire with dignity and security. That's why in Congress Chris is fighting to protect and expand Social Security.
The Union Way of Life
Chris stands with the labor movement and supports the union way of life. At Pitt he fought for a union with the Steelworkers, and in Congress he will always fight to protect hard-earned pensions and outsourcing of jobs.
Good Healthcare
Chris believes that healthcare is a human right. The way he sees it, it is obscene that some Americans cannot afford healthcare一or are stuck paying way too much for lousy care.
Safer Communities
Chris is fighting for a safer Western PA. He supports law enforcement, mental health investments, and common sense gun reforms to keep weapons of war out of our communities and out of the hands of people who are dangers to themselves or others.
Energy Policy and the Climate Crisis
Chris believes that we need bold and swift action to make Western Pennsylvania communities resilient to the effects of climate change and to secure our children’s future on earth.
Protecting His Fellow Veterans
During Chris’s deployment to Iraq, he saw firsthand一like too many of his fellow veterans一the consequences of a war that we never should have fought.
Taking on Corporate Power
For far too long, we have allowed corporations to gain unhealthy power over so much of society and our lives. In Congress, Chris is fighting back against out-of-control corporate power.
