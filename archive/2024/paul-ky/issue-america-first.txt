America First
We spend billions and billions of dollars sending money to countries that hate us. Why should the American taxpayer be on the hook for other countries?
Our taxpayer dollars have gone to fund a cricket team in Pakistan, fund home mortgages in Nigeria, and help people learn Facebook in Kenya.
Instead of sending our money abroad, we should be focusing on what’s happening here at home.
Simply put, it’s time to put our country first.
