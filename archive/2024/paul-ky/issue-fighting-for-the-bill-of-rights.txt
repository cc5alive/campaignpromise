Fighting for the Bill of Rights
We need to become the party of the ENTIRE Bill of Rights. We must defend the entire Constitution with the same furor and the same passion that we defend the Second Amendment. Our liberties are under attack now more than ever. The federal government keeps expanding its power, and our fellow Americans seem increasingly comfortable with trading their liberty for security.
I will defend all of the liberties that we are guaranteed by our nation’s Constitution: our right to free speech, our right to bear arms, and every other enumerated protection that we have been given to protect us from an overreaching federal government.
