Foreign Policy & Defense
I believe in Ronald Reagan’s “Peace through Strength.”
America must protect our interests and our citizens. The decision to enter war should not be taken lightly and must be done only as the Constitution requires: with a formal declaration from Congress.
Over the last few decades, America has wasted millions of dollars and thousands of lives on unchecked, unilateral, never-ending “democracy building” overseas.
We must restore Congressional oversight by ending the dangerous AUMF policy that allows our government to commit American lives and money without approval, so we can increase transparency, protect our troops, and prevent unnecessary war.
