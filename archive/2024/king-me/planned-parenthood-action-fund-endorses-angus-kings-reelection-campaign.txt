Planned Parenthood Action Fund Endorses Angus King’s Reelection Campaign
King has been a longstanding advocate for women to make their own healthcare and family planning decisions
BRUNSWICK, ME
— U.S. Senator Angus King’s reelection campaign today is announcing that it has received the endorsement of Planned Parenthood Action Fund, a key organization in the fight for women’s reproductive and medical freedoms.
“This election year, reproductive freedom is on the ballot and Mainers must do everything they can to keep the government out of the doctor’s office. The first step is electing champions who will fight for their fundamental rights and freedom at every level of government. Angus King is the leader the people of Maine deserve to ensure that people can make their own decisions about abortion care and family planning. Planned Parenthood Action Fund is proud to endorse Angus King, who will continue to be a stalwart champion for women in Maine and across the country,”
said Alexis McGill Johnson, president and CEO, Planned Parenthood Action Fund
.
In response to the endorsement, Angus said:
“As we all know, an examination room is small. There is only enough space for the medical professional and the patient—so we politicians better let those in the room make the best decisions for their family.
“Since the Dobbs decision, I’ve been working with my colleagues in the Senate and the House to restore the protections of Roe into law. There’s a lot at stake in this election: a woman’s ability to make her own health care decisions shouldn’t depend on her zip code, it should be a right. Thank you to Planned Parenthood Action Fund for its support and endorsement. Now, let’s get back to work.”
Senator King is a strong supporter of a woman’s right to choose her own reproductive health care. He
opposed
the Supreme Court’s overturning of
Roe v. Wade
calling it “a dangerous, blatantly political ruling that will rob millions of women the fundamental right to make decisions about their own health, safety, and lives.” Prior to the Supreme Court’s ruling, King voted to advance the
Women’s Health Protection Act
, legislation which would codify the longstanding precedent of
Roe v. Wade
into federal law and protect the right to these vital healthcare decisions.
Most recently, he cosponsored the
Right to Contraception Act
to guarantee the right for people to obtain and use contraceptives, and for health care providers to prescribe contraceptives and make available information related to contraception, free from government interference.
Last spring, he
sent a letter
to the Secretary of Defense highlighting the wide-ranging implications of the Supreme Court’s reversal of
Roe v. Wade
, and the challenges that restrictive abortion laws pose to servicemember health and finances, military readiness, recruitment and retention. He also joined
bipartisan legislation
to ensure military families receive the same access to quality reproductive health care as most Americans.
