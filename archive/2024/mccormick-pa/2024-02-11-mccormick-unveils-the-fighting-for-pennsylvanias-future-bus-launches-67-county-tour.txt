McCormick Unveils the “Fighting for Pennsylvania’s Future“ Bus, Launches 67-County Tour
February 11, 2024
In the News
LITITZ, PENN. —
Dave McCormick, a combat veteran and Pennsylvania job creator who served in the highest levels of government, yesterday unveiled the “Fighting for Pennsylvania’s Future“ bus at a rally with supporters in Lancaster County.
Over the next nine months, Dave will travel on the “Fighting for Pennsylvania’s Future“ Bus to communities in all 67 of Pennsylvania’s counties and meet with thousands of voters across the commonwealth.
“This is the next phase of the campaign — to go make the case to the great people of our commonwealth about the kind of leadership we need to get our country back on track. This is the most important election of our lifetime and I couldn’t be prouder to be running as the Republican nominee to beat Bob Casey in this race for the United States Senate. Join us in this fight to take back our commonwealth and our country,”
said McCormick
.
McCormick was joined by Congressman Lloyd Smucker, State Treasurer Stacy Garrity, State Senator Ryan Aument, State Representative Russ Diamond, and candidate for PA Attorney General Dave Sunday.
