Border Security
Ryan Zinke knows our nation is not secure while our border is wide open under the leadership of “Border Czar” Kamala Harris and Joe Biden. That’s why he’s voted for the strongest border security legislation to pass the house in modern history and has continually voted to hold the Biden-Harris Administration accountable for their actions.
Under Kamala Harris’ policies, there is essentially is no southern border. Cartels, human traffickers, drug smugglers and even terrorists have walked across our border with the millions of other migrants with no enforcement whatsoever. Public hospitals are going bankrupt, law enforcement overrun, and our people are dying because of the invasion. And Monica Tranel would be just another rubber stamp to support open borders if she were elected. She even told us in 2022 that closing the border would have no impact on fentanyl in Montana and accused people who support border security as being racist.
Ryan Zinke has fought for some of the strongest border legislation in history. He voted to force would-be immigrants to wait in Mexico while their immigration application is processed, end abuse of our asylum system which is being exploited by criminals and cartels, deport criminal illegal aliens who rape and murder our people, and finally finish building the wall.
Zinke also introduced legislation giving Tribal police more tools to go after cartel connected drug smugglers and secured federal funding for Montana law enforcement to crack down on the Mexican cartels now operating openly from Bozeman to Browning.
