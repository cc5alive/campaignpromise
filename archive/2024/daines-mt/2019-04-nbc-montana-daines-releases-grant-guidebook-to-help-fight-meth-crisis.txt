Daines releases grant guidebook to help fight meth crisis
Sen. Steve Daines unveiled a new tool to help Montana organizations fight the meth crisis plaguing our state.
He released a guidebook to show available federal funding that can be used in that fight. It shows grants available for addressing addiction, prevention and treatment, law enforcement and also tribal communities.
