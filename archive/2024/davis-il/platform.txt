LIST OF ISSUES
Education
- Quality Education for All: Congressman Davis is committed to ensuring that every child has access to a quality education, regardless of zip code or socioeconomic status.
- Investing in Public Schools: He will fight to increase funding for public schools, support teachers and educators, and expand access to early childhood education and affordable higher education.
Criminal Justice Reform
"The best protection against recidivism is a job"
- Danny K. Davis
-
Ending Mass Incarceration:
Congressman Davis will champion efforts to reform our criminal justice system, focusing on alternatives to incarceration, rehabilitation programs, and reentry services for formerly incarcerated individuals.
- Police Accountability and Community Policing:
He will work to promote accountability and transparency in law enforcement, support community policing initiatives, and advocate for reforms to address systemic racism and bias in policing.
Housing
Affordable Housing:
Danny K. Davis is dedicated to expanding access to affordable housing for all Americans.
Homelessness Prevention:
Congressman Davis will prioritize homelessness prevention efforts by supporting programs that provide housing assistance, wraparound services, and supportive housing for those experiencing homelessness.
Tenant Protections:
Danny K. Davis will work to strengthen tenant protections and prevent unjust evictions by supporting rent control measures, tenant advocacy programs, and affordable housing initiatives.
Fair Housing:
Congressman Davis is committed to fighting housing discrimination and promoting fair housing practices.
Sustainable Housing:
Danny K. Davis will advocate for sustainable housing development practices, including transit-oriented development, energy-efficient housing, and green building initiatives to promote environmental sustainability and affordability.
Social Justice and Civil Rights
- Protecting Civil Rights:
Congressman Davis will defend civil rights and liberties for all Americans, including protections against discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or disability.
LGBTQ+ Equality:
He will support LGBTQ+ rights and advocate for policies that promote equality, inclusion, and acceptance in all aspects of society.
Economic Empowerment
​
- Job Creation and Fair Wages:
Congressman Davis will advocate for policies that stimulate job growth, support small businesses, and ensure that all workers receive fair wages and benefits.
- Wealth and Income Inequality:
He will work to close the wealth gap by implementing progressive tax reforms, expanding access to affordable housing, and investing in programs that promote economic mobility for low-income and minority communities.
Healthcare
for All
- Universal Healthcare Coverage:
Congressman Davis supports the establishment of a universal healthcare system that guarantees access to quality, affordable healthcare for all Americans.
- Mental Health and Substance Abuse:
He will prioritize mental health and substance abuse treatment and prevention initiatives, ensuring that individuals receive the care and support they need to lead healthy and fulfilling lives.
Environment
-
Addressing Climate Change:
Congressman Davis recognizes the urgent need to combat climate change and will support bold initiatives to reduce carbon emissions, invest in renewable energy, and protect our natural resources.
- Environmental Health in Communities:
He will advocate for policies that address environmental injustices, including pollution in marginalized communities, and promote clean air, water, and land for all.
