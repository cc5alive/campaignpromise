LEGISLATIVE ACCOMPLISHMENTS
Campaign Ethics Reform
A former elected official, now registered as a lobbyist, may not use funds from their campaign account to make a political contribution or lobby expenditures.
If they do, they are prohibited from lobbying and registering as a lobbyist for the next 2 years.
Protecting Victims of Child Sex Abuse
Extends the statute of limitations for personal injury claims resulting from child sexual abuse from 15 years to 30 years, allowing a more reasonable period for victims to bring such claims against their offender.
Combating Mail-In Ballot Fraud
Elevated mail-in ballot fraud penalties for those who knowingly and deceive, mislead, or misinform voters.
Deregulated Natural Hair Braiding
Removed the requirement for hair-braiders to obtain a full cosmetology license to braid hair (something the license doesn't even teach!).
Molly Jane's Law
Instructs all law enforcement departments utilize ViCAP - the Violent Criminal Apprehension Program – for sexual assault incident and investigative reporting. ViCAP is designed exclusively to assist in identifying patterns of serial sexual offenders and will assist detectives in quickly identifying potential behavioral patterns in sexually violent predators.
