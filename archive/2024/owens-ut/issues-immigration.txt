Immigration
President Biden’s policies have created a national security and humanitarian crisis at our southern border. Democrat politicians have turned a blind eye as the crisis impacts communities all across the country. America’s national security should not be used as a political pawn to further an out-of-touch agenda. With record amounts of fentanyl pouring into our country at rates never seen before, we need to secure the border.
I am proud to have cosponsored
H.R. 6184
, the
Halt Fentanyl Act
, legislation that would change the scheduling of fentanyl, allowing researchers to study the dangerous public health crisis caused by this deadly drug in our communities.
I have also cosponsored
H.R. 5854
, the
Illegal Immigrant Payoff Prohibition Act
, legislation that prohibits payments of compromise settlements arising out of certain violations of the immigration laws.
I cosponsored
H.R. 1496
,
Sarah’s Law
, a bill that would require an illegal alien present in the US charged with a crime resulting in the death or serious injury of another person to be immediately detained.
I also co-authored a
letter
to Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas requesting information on how DHS plans to secure the border in light of lifting Title 42 expulsion authority.
I am also proud to have co-sponsored
H.R. 6122
, the
Protect American Taxpayer Dollars from Illegal Immigration Act
, which would prevent the federal government from paying settlements to migrant families detained at the border.
