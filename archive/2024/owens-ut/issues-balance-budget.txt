Balance Budget
Every person deserves the opportunity to pursue the American dream. The Biden administration’s policies are making that dream nearly impossible for the average American to achieve. Skyrocketing inflation, reckless government spending, and over-burdensome regulations are stifling innovation and preventing entrepreneurs from achieving success. I will continue to support legislation that empowers business owners, grows the economy, and allows hard-working Americans to keep more of the money they earn.
