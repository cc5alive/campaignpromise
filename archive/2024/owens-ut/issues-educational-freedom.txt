Educational Freedom
Proper education is the key to the middle class and a prosperous future. Every student, no matter their zip code, race, or income level, deserves proper education. I am proud to be a member of the Education and Labor Committee and Ranking Member of the Early Childhood, Elementary, and Secondary Education Subcommittee. I am dedicated to improving our educational system for students’ future and the future for all.
