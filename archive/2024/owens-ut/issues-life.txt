Life
The right to life is the most fundamental of all rights bestowed by God on mankind. To us falls the responsibility to protect the most vulnerable among us and to preserve the God-given rights of those unable to defend themselves.
As a father of six children and 16 grandchildren, I am an unequivocal advocate for life. This issue is deeply personal to me, and I will always stand for the unborn.
