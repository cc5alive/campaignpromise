2nd Amendment
We, as citizens of the United States, have a right granted by God and protected by the Constitution to arm and protect ourselves. The right to bear arms is a fundamental truth outlined in the 2nd amendment and I will continue to fight against laws that aim to regulate our constitutionally provided liberty.
As your Representative, I have a proven record upholding your second amendment rights, and I promise to defend them as long as I am in Congress.
I sent a
letter to the ATF
in response to their attempts to restrict the ability of law-abiding Americans to use silencers on firearms. I will continue to fight ATF overreach and any of their attempts to restrict your second amendment rights.
I co-sponsored
H.R. 1532
, the
Protecting the Right to Keep and Bear Arms Act
, a bill that would prevent any President from imposing stricter gun control measures on law-abiding citizens.
I co-sponsored
H.R. 3130
, the
Repealing Illegal Freedom and Liberty Excises (RIFLE) Act
, which would repeal excise taxes on the transfer of firearms. This bill is a step in the right direction to removing federal red tape for any American wanting to exercise their second amendment rights.
I was proud to vote NO on
H.R. 8 and H.R. 1446
, legislation that would unfairly punish law-abiding citizens, while doing nothing to enforce current laws to keep firearms out of the hands of criminals.
