Healthcare
I believe Utahns deserve health care that is affordable and accessible. I am proud to have co-sponsored and supported many pieces of legislation that reduce government red tape that adds extra layers of complexity and increases the cost of care. It is my goal to improve health care outcomes for all Utahns, and I will continue to support legislation that furthers that goal, including:
