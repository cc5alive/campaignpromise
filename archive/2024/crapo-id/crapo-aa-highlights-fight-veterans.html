Crapo Ad Highlights Fight For Our Veterans

Oct 14, 2016 | Uncategorized

October 14, 2016

Boise, ID – Mike Crapo’s campaign announces the release of its new television ad, “Veteran Testimonial”, which is running statewide. In the ad, Doug Pugmire, who served in the US Air Force, shares his struggles with the VA health care system and Senator Crapo’s successful efforts to help him receive the care that he needed.

“We are blessed to live in a country with brave Americans who embody the truest form of service and who risk everything to protect our freedom,” said Senator Mike Crapo. “I love our veterans, and cannot convey strongly enough my appreciation for their service. Every day, I fight to ensure that our veterans receive the benefits they earned and deserve.”
