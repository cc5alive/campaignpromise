Julie’s Record of Results for North Dakota

Julie knows it’s not good enough just to talk about our conservative values; we must live them. During her time at the Public Service Commission, Julie stood up to the Obama administration to make sure the Dakota Access Pipeline was completed and put into service.

She has continued this fight against the Biden Administration and its war on the coal and oil industry. She has worked hard to maintain reliable and affordable energy in North Dakota. Congress, Julie will work with President Trump to secure North Dakota’s energy and agricultural future.
