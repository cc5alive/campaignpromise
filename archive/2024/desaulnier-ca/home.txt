Meet Mark DeSaulnier.
- - - - - - - - - - - - -
Since 2023, Mark DeSaulnier has represented California’s 10th Congressional District, which covers almost all of Contra Costa County and parts of Alameda County. Previously, he represented the 11th Congressional District for 8 years until redistricting in 2022. He is a
leader
in Congress on the issues that matter most to
California families
, like guaranteeing a livable wage, affordable and accessible education,
and ensuring government is accountable to the public
.
