Thank you for joining Team Moulton!

Since taking office in 2015, Congressman Seth Moulton and his team have been hard at work making a difference for the constituents of Massachusetts’ 6th congressional district and people across the country. We’re proud of all the work we’ve done and look forward to tackling the challenges that lie ahead.

Congressman Moulton’s priorities include advocating for quality and accessible mental health care, combating climate change, ending the scourge of gun violence, bolstering public transportation, and ensuring the national security of our country. Congressman Moulton’s political organization, Serve America, supports service-oriented candidates running for federal office. All are impressive and inspiring leaders.

If you’re interested in volunteer opportunities with Seth’s re-election campaign or if you would like to get in touch with Seth Moulton's political team in MA-06 or Serve America, please call (617) 209-9816.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Congressman Seth Moulton saw the consequences of failed leadership in Washington first-hand over the course of his four tours as a Marine in Iraq. In 2017 he founded Serve America to help elect a new generation of service–driven leaders from military, national security, and public service backgrounds who are willing to put the country first and take on the tough challenges in Washington.
