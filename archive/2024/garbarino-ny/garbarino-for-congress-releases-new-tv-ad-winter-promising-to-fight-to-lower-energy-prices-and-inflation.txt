GARBARINO FOR CONGRESS RELEASES NEW TV AD “WINTER” PROMISING TO FIGHT TO LOWER ENERGY PRICES AND INFLATION
SAYVILLE, NY –
Garbarino for Congress today released a NEW TV ad ahead of the 2022 general election,
“Winter.”
“Winter” is a 30-second TV ad that underscores Andrew Garbarino’s commitment to fighting Washington Democrats’ excessive spending and anti-American energy policies that have skyrocketed costs for Long Island families. Andrew supports restoring American energy independence and unleashing more domestic oil and gas production in order to bring down gas prices and home heating costs. Re-electing Andrew is critical to addressing the energy and inflation crisis by helping Republicans take back the Majority and firing Nancy Pelosi as Speaker of the House.
Andrew has been a consistent voice in support of reducing overregulation that has hindered economic recovery, increased our reliance of foreign resources, and raised costs for families. As a lifelong Long Islander, Andrew will continue to fight to address the economic challenges facing our community.
The ad will run district-wide on cable TV, connected TV, and digital platforms.
Congressman Garbarino was first elected to Congress in 2020 in a hotly contested race to succeed retiring Congressman Peter King. The former private attorney and New York State Assemblyman is a member of the House Homeland Security and Small Business Committee. He resides in Bayport, NY.
