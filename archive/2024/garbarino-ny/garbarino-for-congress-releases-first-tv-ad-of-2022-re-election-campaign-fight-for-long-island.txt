Garbarino for Congress Releases First TV Ad of 2022 Re-Election Campaign, “Fight for Long Island”
SAYVILLE, NY
– Garbarino for Congress today released the first TV ad of Congressman Andrew R. Garbarino’s 2022 re-election campaign, “
Fight for Long Island
.”
“Fight for Long Island”
is a 30-second TV ad that underscores Andrew Garbarino’s deep ties to the community he serves, his credentials as a strong conservative Republican, and his commitment to fighting for Long Islanders and against the liberal Biden agenda. Re-electing Andrew is critical to helping Republicans take back the Majority and firing Nancy Pelosi as Speaker of the House.
Andrew has consistently voted in Congress against President Biden and Speaker Pelosi’s radical spending agenda and supported legislation to secure our border, stand with our brave law enforcement officers, and restore our local economy. As a lifelong Long Islander, Andrew will continue to fight to lower taxes, defend the middle class, support small businesses, protect our environment, and stand up for our veterans.
The ad will run district-wide on cable TV, connected TV, and digital platforms.
Congressman Garbarino was first elected to Congress in 2020 in a hotly contested race to succeed retiring Congressman Peter King. The former private attorney and New York State Assemblyman is a member of the House Homeland Security and Small Business Committee. He resides in Bayport, NY. Learn more here:
garbarinoforny.com
