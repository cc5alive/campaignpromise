      Voting Information for the 2024 General Election

      Sacramento County

      Sacramento County My Voter Portal

      Here you can view your County Voter Information Guide, find your nearest Vote Center and more.

      https://myvoterportal.saccounty.gov/LookupVoter

      Vote By Mail and Official Ballot Drop Box Locations

      ​​Every registered voter in the County of Sacramento will be mailed a ballot starting 29 days before Election Day.  Find more information on Vote by Mail ballots and the different ways you can return​ your voted ballot.

      https://elections.saccounty.net/VotebyMail/Pages/default.aspx

      Vote Centers – Vote Early or on Election Day

      California law allows voters to visit any Vote Center in the County of Sacramento to drop off their voted ballot, use one of three accessible ballot marking devices, obtain voting assistance, or receive a replacement ballot. 

      https://elections.saccounty.net/VotebyMail/Pages/Voting_Before_Election_Day.aspx

      Yolo County

      Vote By Mail and Official Ballot Drop Box Locations

      Voters may return their voted ballot by mail, at a Vote Center, or at an official Ballot Drop Box (BDB) open throughout Yolo County.

      https://ace.yolocounty.gov/305/Vote-Centers-Ballot-Drop-Boxes-Locations
