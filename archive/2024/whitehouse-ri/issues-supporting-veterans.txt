Supporting Veterans
We can never fully repay the debt of gratitude we owe to servicemembers and military families who sacrifice so much for our nation. I am working hard to ensure our troops have everything they need to come back safe, and that there’s a place to call home, a job, and excellent health care here for them when they return. I was a big supporter of the PACT Act, which expanded health benefits for veterans who got sick because of toxic exposures during their service to the country.
