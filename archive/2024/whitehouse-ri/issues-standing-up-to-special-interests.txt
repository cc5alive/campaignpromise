Standing Up to Special Interests
The American people are fed up with dark money influence campaigns that allow billionaires and big corporations to blockade progress on essential needs like solving climate change, enacting commonsense gun safety laws, and restoring women’s reproductive freedom. We need to root out special interest influence from our government and our courts, and restore power to ordinary Americans. I’m leading the charge in the Senate for disclosure laws that would undo the disastrous Citizens United decision and prevent billionaires from anonymously pulling the strings of government from the shadows.
