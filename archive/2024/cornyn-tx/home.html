Meet John

Senator Cornyn is a strong leader with a proven record of accomplishments. Whether it’s fighting for Hurricane Harvey relief, working to strengthen the criminal background check system to ensure convicted felons and domestic abusers cannot illegally purchase a firearm, or his dedication to providing much-needed resources to reduce the backlog of untested rape kits — Senator Cornyn has delivered for all Texans.

  • Sponsoring and passing the Justice for Victims of Trafficking Act – cracking down on human trafficking and providing care and justice to survivors, especially children, of trafficking 
  • Sponsoring and passing the Fix NICS Act – a bipartisan effort supporting domestic violence and law enforcement groups that enhances compliance and ensures accuracy of existing background check systems for firearm purchases
  • Passing the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act – helping families and businesses keep more of what they earn
  • Authoring the Affordable Prescriptions for Patients Act – lowering the cost of prescription drugs by preventing pharmaceutical companies from gaming the patent system
