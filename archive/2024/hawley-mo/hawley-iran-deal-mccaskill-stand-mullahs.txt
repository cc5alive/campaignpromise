Hawley On Iran Deal: Does McCaskill stand with the Mullahs?
LEBANON, Mo. –
In response to President Trump’s announcement about withdrawing from the Iran Deal, Missouri U.S. Senate candidate Josh Hawley issued the following statement:
“President Trump made the right decision to rip up the Iran deal. President Obama and Secretary Kerry never should have cut this deal in the first place, and Senator McCaskill never should have approved it.
“We should be standing with President Trump and Israel today. If you aren’t, you are standing with the mullahs and John Kerry. Senator McCaskill needs to make it clear that she stands with President Trump and Israel, and not the mullahs.”
