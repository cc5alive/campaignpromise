Trump spelled out his terrifying plans for a second term
Time Magazine
just released its interview with Donald Trump, where he lays out his dangerous plans for what would be a catastrophic second term.
While we all need to work like hell to keep him out of the White House,
I'm

In the interview, Trump says he wants to enact a myriad of horrifying policies including:
Building migrant detention camps and forcing through a plan to round up and mass deport 11 million people from the country
Letting states monitor women's pregnancies and criminally prosecute anyone who violates unjust and harmful abortion bans
Firing any U.S. attorney who doesn't carry out his order to prosecute someone
Pardoning every one of his insurrectionist supporters who attacked the Capitol on Jan. 6
Filling his Administration with acolytes who will support his damaging agenda and authoritarian tendencies no matter what
Make no mistake: Trump remains the same person he was when he was first elected. He's still a self-aggrandizing, revenge-seeking danger to our country.
We can't allow him to get anywhere near the White House this November. We need to keep building the grassroots momentum it will take to run up the numbers across the country.
This November, we have a choice:
Will we sit on the sidelines and watch MAGA Republicans take over our government, or will we take action and fight for a better future for generations to come?
I'll be defending the Democratic Senate Majority, and today, I'm asking for your support to make sure our incredible Democratic incumbents have the resources they need as soon as possible to win in November.
If we win big in key Senate races across the country, that means we also turned out enough Democratic voters to keep President Biden in the White House, too.
Will you join me in the fight to defend the Democratic Senate Majority from a total MAGA takeover by pitching in whatever you can today?
