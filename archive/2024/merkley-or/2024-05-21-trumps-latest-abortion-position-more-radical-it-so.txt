Trump’s latest abortion position is more radical than it sounds
Donald Trump has been talking about abortion a lot lately. He says he won't sign a federal abortion ban, but does anyone actually believe him?
We're fighting to defend the Democratic Senate Majority to ensure a national abortion ban NEVER passes. Will you join us in the fight by pitching in to The Blue Wave Project, which helps save the Senate from a MAGA takeover?
While Trump is distorting his record and distracting voters on the issue of abortion, we must not let him – or the MAGA Republicans in Congress – rewrite history to claim that they wouldn't take an active approach to banning abortion nationwide.
If elected in November, Trump will re-enter office with broad executive authority to restrict abortion access.
We can't trust him with the power to further gut access to reproductive rights.
Trump himself has said he wants to let states monitor women's pregnancies and criminally prosecute anyone who violates unjust and harmful abortion bans!
We must stop Trump because we can't allow him to get anywhere near the White House this November. Reproductive freedom for millions depends on it.
Today, I'm asking for your support to make sure our incredible Democratic incumbents have the resources they need as soon as possible to win in November.
If we win big in key Senate races across the country, that means we also turned out enough Democratic voters to protect our rights and keep President Biden in the White House, too.
Will you join me in the fight to defend the Democratic Senate Majority from a total MAGA takeover and help defend reproductive rights across the country by pitching in today?
