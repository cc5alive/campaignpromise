Kaine For Virginia Campaign Launches Final Closing TV Ad, “Faith And Freedom,” Highlighting That Senator Kaine Is Standing Up For All Virginians
Senator Kaine: “Wherever you live and work, whether we agree or disagree, the freedom to be who we are while respecting others makes America special.”
Richmond, VA
– The Kaine for Virginia campaign is launching our final TV ad, “Faith and Freedom,” highlighting Senator Kaine’s work throughout the years to stand up for all Virginians.
WATCH
:
The ad begins with Senator Kaine reciting James 2:18, “I’ll show you my faith by my works,” to show how Senator Kaine’s faith guides his public service. The ad spotlights that Senator Kaine is improving the lives of all Virginians, no matter what they believe or where they live.
“Senator Kaine’s faith has been a guiding light for him throughout his public service career,”
said Michael Beyer, Communications Director for Kaine for Virginia.
“Senator Kaine is working for all Virginians, to show up for them and stand up for them in the U.S. Senate. This new ad drives the point home that no matter where you live, what you believe, or even if you disagree with him, Senator Kaine is working to make the lives of Virginians better.”
Watch the ad
here
and view a full transcript below:
Senator Tim Kaine
: I’m Tim Kaine.
When I served with missionaries in Honduras, I embraced the bible verse “I’ll show you my faith by my works.”
My work is making life better for all Virginians.
Through the years I’ve showed up for you and stood up for you.
Wherever you live and work, whether we agree or disagree, the freedom to be who we are while respecting others makes America special.
That’s why I approve this message.
