Meet Jodey

Jodey was raised in the heart of West Texas as the son of a tractor salesman and a schoolteacher. As a graduate of Texas Tech University, his passion for public service and leadership shaped his career in the Office of the Texas Governor, the White House, the FDIC, and later as President of a healthcare innovation company and Vice Chancellor of the Texas Tech University System in Lubbock, Texas.

 

In January of 2017, Jodey became only the fifth person to represent Texas’ 19th Congressional District since its formation in 1935. At the beginning of the 118th Congress, he was elected to serve as Chairman of the House Budget Committee. Now in his 4th term, Jodey is focused on leading the charge to “Reverse the Curse” of our national debt along with changing the course and cultural of Washington as a senior member of the House Ways and Means Committee and various leadership roles in the House. 


﻿﻿He is honored to serve the good people of West Texas and is especially thankful for the tremendous sacrifice and support of his wife, Anne, and their three children.
