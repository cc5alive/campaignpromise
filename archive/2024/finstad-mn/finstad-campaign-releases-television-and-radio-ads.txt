New Ulm, MN
– The Finstad for Congress Campaign today released television and radio ads in the run up to the Special General Election on Tuesday, August 9.
The television and radio ads highlight Brad Finstad’s agricultural background as a farmer and his roots in the 1
st
Congressional District and provides voters with a contrast between Finstad and his opponent, Jeff Ettinger, who has donated thousands of dollars to Democrats and supports the Biden-Pelosi agenda.
“Minnesota families have been devastated by the Biden-Pelosi policies. Minnesotans cannot afford to pay more for gas, food, necessities and more government spending. It is time for change in Washington, D.C. and that’s what we’ll deliver when we win on Tuesday, August 9,”
said Finstad.
