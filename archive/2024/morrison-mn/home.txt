Meet Kelly
Friends,
I’ve spent most of my career as a doctor – not a politician.
I know what it’s like to see my patients struggle to afford the medications they need, so I took on Big Pharma and lowered prescription drug costs. I know how important it is that women – not politicians – are able to make their own health care decisions, so I led the effort to protect abortion rights in Minnesota.
I believe that we need leaders who are focused on delivering results. I’ve worked with both Democrats and Republicans to pass over 90 bills in six years, more than almost every other legislator at the Minnesota capitol.
I’m running for Congress because I want to take this common-sense, results-focused approach to Washington and deliver for the people in the Third District.
I hope you will join my team.
With gratitude,
Kelly
