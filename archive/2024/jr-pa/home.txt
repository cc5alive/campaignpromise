New Leadership for Northeastern Pennsylvania
Meet
Rob Bresnahan
A fifth-generation Northeastern Pennsylvanian, Rob Bresnahan took the reins of his grandfather’s union electrical contracting company at age 19 and expanded the business across the commonwealth while tripling the number of good-paying union careers they provided to Pennsylvanians. Rob is focused on facing challenges head on to solve problems.
That’s why he’s running for United States Congress.
Rob is committed to bringing people together and is uniquely qualified to send a jolt of energy through Congress and deliver results for everyone here in Northeastern Pennsylvania.

Issues
Border
We must secure the border, build the wall, and reverse the Biden/Harris failed policies to stop the flow of illegal drugs and criminals into our communities.
Economy
Northeastern PA families are hurting in the Biden/Harris economy. We need to stop the reckless spending, get inflation under control, and cut taxes so you can keep more of the money you earn for your family.
Social Security & Medicare
I will always fight to protect Social Security & Medicare for future generations. It’s your money, you paid into the system, and the government needs to keep its promise to every American. I will not support raising the retirement age or any benefit cuts to these programs.
Public Safety
I will always stand with law enforcement and ensure they have the resources to combat the fentanyl crisis and keep our community safe. For years as a private citizen I have donated to and supported our local police and in Congress I will always back the men and women that keep our families and communities safe.
Jobs and the Economy
As a small business owner, I know what it takes to create good, family-sustaining jobs. I will vote for commonsense policies that unleash the American economy and get Pennsylvania back to work.
Your Voice in Congress
I am running for Congress because career politicians have forgotten about people back home. I was born and raised here (and I even have my plot in the Forty Fort cemetery reserved) and I will always be accessible to constituents and be a tireless advocate for our community.
