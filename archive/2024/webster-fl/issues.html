List of Services

  • AMERICA FIRST

    Count on Dan Webster to always support and  push an America First Agenda. America must  stop being the world care giver and protect its  people and borders.

    STAND WITH DAN List Item 1
  • BORDER SECURITY

    Dan Webster believes border security is national  security. He championed President Trump’s  efforts to build the wall and continues fighting to  secure our southwest border. 

    STAND WITH DAN List Item 2
  • LESS GOVERNMENT SPENDING

    Leading by example Dan Webster has voluntarily  reduced his Congressional salary, has refused  government-subsidized health care, refused  to travel abroad on taxpayers’ expense. By  reducing his official Congressional office budget,  Dan Webster personally returned over $4 million  to the U.S. Treasury.

    STAND WITH DAN List Item 3
  • SERVING VETERANS

    Dan Webster is committed to making sure veterans  have access to the care and services they have  earned. He has introduced and supported bills  that reform, protect, and expand VA services.  Working with VA services he has gotten more  than $10 million returned to Florida veterans in  compensation, pension or retroactive benefits.

    STAND WITH DAN List Item 4
  • SERVING SENIORS

    Dan Webster is committed to ensuring America  keeps the promise of Social Security so seniors  don’t have the rug pulled out from under them.  

    He also pushed legislation to end the double-tax  on social security benefits. 

    STAND WITH DAN
  • RIGHT TO BEAR ARMS

    Dan Webster is a steadfast supporter and  advocate of Second Amendment rights. He  successfully fought the ATF’s unlawful gun  control attempts. A recipient of the 2022  Defender of Freedom Award from the NRA  Golden Eagles, Dan can be trusted to keep  supporting American’s right to bear arms. 


    STAND WITH DAN
  • PROTECTING FAMILY & LIFE

    Dan Webster is a well-known pro-life advocate.  Life begins at conception and the protection of  life ought to begin at conception as well. The  current system of infanticide can no longer be  tolerated under any legal or scientific standard.
