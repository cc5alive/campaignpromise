Young Kim Focuses on Veterans Homelessness in New Television Ad
La Habra, CA — This morning Young Kim for Congress released its second television ad focusing on Kim’s years of work in the 39th Congressional District and her fight to get funding to address Veterans homelessness while serving in the California State Assembly. The thirty-second spot, called “Exactly Who We Need”, features retiring Representative Ed Royce speaking about Young Kim’s passion for public service and concluding that “Young Kim is exactly who we need representing us.”
Watch the ad “Exactly Who We Need” below:
The full transcript of the ad can be found below:
Young Kim: I’m Young Kim and I approve this message.
Ed Royce: I’ll tell you about Young Kim.
She has a deep passion for public service – because she feels her success is owed to the heroes who provided her the freedom to succeed.
So when she came to me, heartbroken, over veterans homelessness I knew that for Young Kim it was personal.
She said: “Ed, politicians want to give sanctuary to illegal immigrants but we aren’t even giving proper sanctuary to our veterans! Well I won’t stand for it!”
I knew then, that Young Kim is exactly who we need representing us.
The ad will run on cable television throughout the 39th Congressional District.
The 39th Congressional District is split between Orange, Los Angeles and San Bernardino counties. To learn more about Young Kim and her campaign please visit
