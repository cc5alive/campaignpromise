Born for public service
Jared Moskowitz is a lifelong public servant who has led our community through tragedy and triumph. As a City Commissioner, Democratic State Representative, Florida’s Director of Emergency Management, Broward County Commissioner, and Congressman, he has always fought to keep residents safe, champion bipartisan solutions and push for reforms that help South Florida families. He is a loving father and husband fighting to create a better world for his family and yours.
