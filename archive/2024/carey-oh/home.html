Meet Mike Carey

Congressman Mike Carey proudly represents Ohio’s 15th Congressional District, encompassing all of Madison county and parts of Clark, Fayette, Franklin, Miami and Shelby counties. Sworn in on November 4, 2021, Mike was able to quickly distinguish himself as an effective member and was appointed to the prestigious House Committee on Ways and Means and the Committee on House Administration in 2023. Additionally, Mike serves as a Deputy Whip and as an NRCC Co-Chair.
