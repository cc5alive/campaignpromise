November 7, 2023
As a sovereign nation, Israel has the right to defend itself and its citizens from terrorist attacks, abductions, and ongoing threats to its security. The October 7th attacks by Hamas on Israeli civilians not only violate international law, but are a grave injustice to be condemned. I urge you to stand with me in supporting Israel’s right to self-defense and to work towards stabilizing the land within its borders to ensure the safety and well-being of all civilians in the region.
The longstanding conflict in the Middle East has caused immense suffering and instability. By standing with Israel we can contribute to a more stable and secure future for the entire region. If you stand with me in support of Israel in its pursuit of security, add your name to the petition below.
