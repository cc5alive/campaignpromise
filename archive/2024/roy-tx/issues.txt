Our U.S. Representative for the Texas 21st must demonstrate a genuine commitment to stand strong against the tidal wave of DC insiders and have a proven track record of doing so – on all issues – across the board.
Standing for America
+
Standing for America
When Jefferson concluded the Declaration of Independence and wrote that “with a firm reliance on the protection of Divine Providence, we mutually pledge to each other our Lives, our Fortunes, and our sacred Honor,” he wasn’t just talking about those 56 men sitting there in Independence Hall. He was talking about all of us who would follow as fellow Americans. It is time for us to live up to that legacy, stand up for America, and take it back!
Standing With Law Enforcement
+
Standing With Law Enforcement
The men and women of Law Enforcement put their lives on the line each and every day to ensure the safety of our communities. In the summer of 2020, as our protectors were under attack from the radical left, I stood on the House floor and read the name of every law enforcement officer killed in the line of duty that year. We cannot do enough to express our gratitude for their sacrifice.
I am proud to stand with them, #BackTheBlue, and fight to ensure they have the resources they need to do their jobs and keep our communities safe. Earlier this year, I was named to House Republicans’ American Security Task Force which is focused on securing our border and our communities. In D.C., I have consistently opposed legislation that would take money and tools away from police officers or make it harder for them to do their job.
Securing the Border
+
Securing the Border
When it comes to immigration, the federal government should have one focus: America. It should first focus its efforts on securing our borders so that terrorists, criminals, and illegal immigrants are not allowed to come and go as they please. That means we need a wall, yes, but far greater interior enforcement to stem the tide of illegal presence. Then it should focus its efforts on designing an immigration system based on American values, not those of special interests and Washington politicians.
Cartels have operational control of our southern border and their violence is permeating into our communities far away from the border. That is why I introduced legislation to designate cartels as the foreign terrorist organizations that they are and penned an
op-ed
with the criminal district attorney of Kendall County to raise awareness about the danger of cartels in our communities.
To ensure our Border Patrol agents can regain operational control of the border, I have introduced legislation to build navigable roads and clear the impenetrable cane along the border to provide clear visibility. Swamp politics have blocked the completion of the border wall. That’s why I offered an amendment that would use the money from corrupt earmarks to complete construction of the wall.
Securing our border could be accomplished with a simple one page bill if politicians simply had the will. Earlier this year I sent a
9-page memo
to “Border Czar” Kamala Harris to lay out the problem and the simple solutions. But she, and her boss, have no plan.

Restoring Healthcare Freedom
+
Restoring Healthcare Freedom
Healthcare freedom ultimately means being able to purchase insurance tailored to one’s specific health needs. We haven’t had it since long before Obamacare – and THAT is the problem. It is high time we are free in this country to contract for healthcare services and healthcare insurance without government mandates that drive up costs and restrict freedom. “Obamacare repeal” is just a (broken) campaign promise.
I am the chair of the Republican Study Committee’s Healthcare Task Force and leading the Republican party in developing conservative, free market solutions to high costs and reduced access that is the result of excessive government control over our healthcare.

Strengthening our Military, Supporting our Veterans
+
Strengthening our Military, Supporting our Veterans
The brave men and women in the military must have what they need to defend our country overseas and here at home. For too many years, our readiness has declined as the threats against our national security have grown. I support strengthening our military and ensuring they have both the tools and the rules of engagement that permits them to succeed on the battlefield. I also support keeping our military focused. I penned an op-ed that described how “wokeism” is weakening our country and creating international threats to our security, and I introduced legislation to eliminate the newly-created Chief Diversity Officer position at DOD, because our military should be focused on producing the most lethal and effective soldiers, not the most diverse.
As a member of the Veterans Affairs Committee, honoring and caring for our Veterans is one of my top priorities in Congress. I was the lead Republican cosponsor of the Forgotten Vietnam Veterans Act, signed into law by President Trump, ensuring all Vietnam veterans have access to the benefits they earned through their sacrifice. I have also introduced legislation to ensure veterans that struggle to get the care they need from the VA can use their benefits to access alternative care of their choice as well as legislation that protects the Second Amendment rights of veterans that have struggled with mental health issues.
