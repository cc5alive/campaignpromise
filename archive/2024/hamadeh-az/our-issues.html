OUR

ISSUES

BORDER SECURITY

Border Security is National Security and I’m proud to have the support of President Trump's National Security Team. Our border is a complete disaster due to the Biden’s administration’s intentional policies. In Congress, I will make border security a top priority by allocating resources to target and end illegal border crossings and crimes at the border. I will work with President Trump to build the wall and will work to pass a bill classifying drug cartels as the terrorist organizations they are. I will always vote to defend our border and protect private property.

ELECTION INTEGRITY

America cannot exist without free and fair elections. What we witnessed in the 2020 and 2022 election should never happen again and there must be accountability. I am still fighting for those that were disenfranchised last November in court and will never waver in fighting for the integrity of our elections. I am going to take this fight to Congress and work to ensure our voting rolls are clean, ranked choice voting is banned at the federal level and both parties have expressed concerns about election security since the turn of the decade. It is not a political issue, but a matter of ensuring our most fundamental rights have not been subverted. I will work to bring accountability to our elections.

COST OF LIVING

Our federal government has been living beyond its means for far too long and hard working families are paying the price with record inflation. The cost of everything has skyrocketed from the gas pumps to the grocery store. In the course of Joe Biden’s presidency, our country has felt the downturn of a recession and the abandonment of common sense by our government. We must return to normalcy by actually getting back to passing a budget. It’s time to have the courage to live within our means and stop bankrupting future generations.

FENTANYL CRISIS

Harmful drugs like Fentanyl are pouring across our borders and into our neighborhoods, poisoning our kids. It is unacceptable. This begins with reclassifying the criminal code so that Fentanyl is a “dangerous” drug or a “poison” whose drug classifications allow for harsher punishment and enhanced sentencing. Criminals must fear consequences for their actions, and in Congress, I will uphold the rule of law and keep our communities SAFE.

CRIME

Crime is out of control, and our cities are falling to ruin. Liberal policies have allowed criminals to act without consequence and degrade civilized society. America is a nation of laws, and we need to enforce those laws. In Congress, I will support funding, backing, and revitalizing police forces across the country. We must enforce existing laws that would punish the criminals causing havoc in our streets.

FOREIGN POLICY

The world is safest when America is strong. The Biden Administration has been a complete failure in projecting American strength and the world is on fire because of it. Their humiliating withdrawal from Afghanistan showed weakness and indecision. It prompted malicious foreign actors around the world to advance their agendas—and the world is far more unstable because of it. In Congress, I will promote peace through strength. We have to reestablish Trump-era sanctions on China and Iran and show our allies we’re still the predominant world power. We must also focus on producing American energy to economically strengthen our country from within, decrease our dependence on unstable foreign regimes, and rebuild the Strategic Petroleum Reserves Joe Biden depleted for political gain.

SUPPORTING OUR VETERANS

As the only veteran in this race and having served overseas, I will fight for the benefits our brave men and women deserve. In Congress, I will be a voice for our active duty personnel and veterans and will seek resources to address the mental health epidemic that is plaguing our veteran community. No veteran should feel hopeless and neglected in America. In addition, I will continue to fight for additional funding for our VA hospitals so every veteran gets the proper health care needed.

PROTECTING SENIORS

Promises made are promises kept. At a time when Americans are hurting and seeing the value of their dollar devalued, we must do everything we can to preserve Social Security for our seniors. I will never support policies that would jeopardize the safety of the program that so many seniors have paid into and the benefits they have earned.

SECOND AMENDMENT

America is built on fundamental rights that we, as citizens, can never lose. Our First Amendment gives us the right to say what we want; The Second Amendment protects the First. I will unequivocally support your right to bear arms and protect the Second Amendment.

PROTECTING LIFE

Abe believes that the most important thing in the world is family and his six young nieces and nephews have taught him that life is a precious gift. Abe is a pro-life advocate that understands this fight goes well beyond the issue of abortion. Abe will always fight for the rights of the unborn, especially against any effort that allows abortion with little to no restrictions. In addition, Abe believes there needs to be a broader focus on making sure all young mothers have the resources and support to raise children. Adoption is expanded so that children have the opportunities to grow up with families that love them, and pregnancy centers are available to those in need.
