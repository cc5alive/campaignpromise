Meet Ayanna
Ayanna Pressley is an advocate, a policy-maker, an activist, and a survivor. Ayanna believes that the people closest to the pain should be closest to the power and that a diversity of voices in the political process is essential to crafting more effective public policy.

Issues that matter to all of us.
Working together, we’ve made vital progress on issues of deep consequence to the communities we call home, from abortion access and reproductive freedom, to student loan cancellation and housing justice. And we’ve delivered more than $35 million in funding to build a more just and equitable district by supporting community health centers, vital transit projects, workforce training, climate justice, and more.
This diverse, intergenerational movement led by the people has proven the change we can make when we organize, mobilize our collective power, and lead with love.
