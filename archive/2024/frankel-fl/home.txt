Meet Lois
Lois Frankel is an experienced public servant with a strong record of bringing jobs and opportunity to South Florida. After serving 14 years in the Florida state legislature, including as the first woman Democratic Minority Leader, Lois became Mayor of West Palm Beach in 2003, where she served for eight years. She is the first woman to represent Florida’s 22nd congressional district and has been representing Palm Beach County since being elected in 2013. In Congress, Lois is a member of the House Appropriations Committee, where she serves on the Subcommittees for Labor, Health and Human Services, Education and State, Foreign Operations. She also serves as the Chair of the Democratic Women’s Caucus, bringing women’s issues to the forefront of all policy decisions.
