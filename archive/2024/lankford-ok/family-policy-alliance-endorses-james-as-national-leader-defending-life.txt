Family Policy Alliance Endorses James as National Leader Defending Life
May 11, 2022
Facebook
Twitter
OKLAHOMA CITY, OK –
Families for James Lankford announced James was endorsed by the Family Policy Alliance, which is the public policy partner of Focus on the Family. FPA’s President and CEO issued a statement to endorse James’s campaign.
“Sen. Lankford has been an incredible ally in the fight for life, religious freedom, and traditional values,” said FPA President & CEO, Craig DeRoche. “It’s why he was honored in 2021 with the Coburn Statesman Award, named after another great Oklahoman and friend of Family Policy Alliance, Tom Coburn.”
“The partnership with Family Policy Alliance is critical to my work on behalf of Oklahomans to protect our conservative values. I am grateful for their continued support and friendship as we protect every life, defend our Constitutional right to live our faith, and protect the values we hold dear. I am honored to have their endorsement as I fight the right way in the US Senate.”
