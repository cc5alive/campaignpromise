Endorsement Alert: Oklahoma Ag Community Stands with James
June 6, 2022
Facebook
Twitter
OKLAHOMA CITY, OK –
Families for James Lankford announced James received another strong endorsement, this time from the Oklahoma Farm Bureau. OKFB Ag PAC joins the almost 100 endorsements James has received from state and national leaders.
Mike Schulz, OKFB Ag PAC Chairman said of the endorsement:
“In an increasingly urban world, it is crucial to have elected officials who understand the importance of the agriculture industry and uphold the values of rural Oklahoma. The Ag PAC is another way Oklahoma Farm Bureau gives a voice to farmers and ranchers through the grassroots process on which our organization is founded.”
“All of us that eat, live in homes, and wear clothes are grateful for the hard work of Oklahoma farmers and ranchers. The Oklahoma ag community is not immune to Biden’s failed policies and the unique challenges of living in rural Oklahoma. Farmers and ranchers deserve a strong fight for their families and against rising costs and supply-chain issues. I am honored to stand with our hard-working Oklahoma farmers and ranchers as they continue to provide for our nation and the world.”
