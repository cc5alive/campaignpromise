As a doctor,
Ami Bera
has always put service first, and he believes that Congress should be a place for service. That’s why Dr. Bera is excited to run in
California’s new 6th congressional district
in order to fight for the things that matter most to Sacramento County families.

On the Issues that Matter
Health Care
Dr. Bera believes in creating access to a healthcare system that provides universal coverage and ensures everyone has access to quality, affordable healthcare.
Veterans
Dr. Bera believes that the mistreatment of veterans is a national tragedy, not to mention immoral and un-American.
Jobs and the Economy
An Economy that encourages competition and innovation.
Energy and the Environment
Central to our national mission is the mandate to build for future generations.
Medicare and Social Security
Dr. Bera believes that Medicare and Social Security are guaranteed promises and the key to a dignified retirement.
National Security
We must be tough and smart not only about challenges we face today, such as securing our borders and fighting terrorism, but also about the emerging threat of nuclear proliferation.
Women’s Equality
Dr. Bera believes that women’s equality, equal pay for equal work, and access to health care are fundamental rights.
