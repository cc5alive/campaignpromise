FIGHTING FOR SOUTHWEST MICHIGAN
As a representative of Michigan, Bill has proven himself to be a leader in the fight to rein in the out-of-control spending that is fueling inflation, restore energy independence, and lower the cost of living for hardworking individuals and families across Southwest Michigan.
Bill’s other areas of focus include, restoring order at the border, limiting the scope of the federal government, protecting the Great Lakes, expanding access to capital formation, and creating a sensible regulatory environment that provides for necessary safety standards without negatively impacting the private sector’s ability to expand and create good-paying jobs.
