BDN: Chellie Pingree asks feds to halt plans for ICE detention facility in Scarborough
Tweet
March 2, 2020
Lawmakers led by Rep. Chellie Pingree, D-Maine, are asking a federal agency to delay the operations of
a planned Immigration and Customs Enforcement facility outside Portland
, which would temporarily detain immigrants suspected of immigration violations.
They are pushing for more transparency, saying that an ICE facility could traumatize veterans who seek services at a veterans health agency slated to operate in the same building. Specifically, they are encouraging ICE to conduct public meetings in Scarborough and present its plans for this facility.
