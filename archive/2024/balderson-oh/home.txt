About
Troy Balderson
We need to get back to normal.
Here’s what’s not normal…
Ohio families are paying more for gas, groceries, and virtually everything else. We’re being punished with higher prices and too many families are now living paycheck to paycheck.
Small businesses are grappling with supply chain delays and higher costs—all while struggling to find enough qualified workers just to keep their doors open.
From big cities to small towns, violent crime is threatening our safety, making our communities more dangerous.
Meanwhile, illegal drugs and people are pouring over our southern border and China and Russia are testing our resolve.
Let’s face it… Joe Biden and the Radical Left in Congress have failed. Now they’re pushing higher taxes and a ridiculous “woke” agenda that is only making matters worse. Their agenda only weakens and divides our country.
Democrats cannot repair what their failed policies have broken.
I’m running for Congress because I believe America is worth fixing. I believe traditional, conservative principles are the way to do it.
I hope you’ll join our campaign to get America back to normal.
Sincerely,
Troy Balderson
