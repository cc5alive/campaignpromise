Securing Our Border

America is the most welcoming country in the world. But when we welcome people to our home, we ask that they meet us at the front door instead of climbing through the window.

  • Finish the wall. 
  • Go after the cartels profiting from the flow of illegal immigrants and drugs. 
  • Invest in better technology to detect illegal crossings.
  • Hire more border patrol officers and fewer IRS agents.
  • Put an end to sanctuary cities, driver’s licenses, and discounted tuition.
  • Stop welfare payments to illegal immigrants.
Unleashing American Energy

Never again can we weaken America by making our country dependent on China, Russia, and other bad actors for our energy needs.

  • Follow the science. Acknowledge natural gas for its leading role in reducing emissions.
  • Unleash the abundant resources available just beneath our feet.
  • Lift the Biden LNG export ban to expand energy markets and support our European allies. 
  • Streamline permits.
  • Stop the radical green agenda, which is distorting facts and forcing pain on consumers.
  • Build pipelines and safeguard America’s energy infrastructure to safely speed up transportation.
  • Allow consumers to choose the vehicle that is best for their own needs.
Stopping the Biden Price Hikes

Joe Biden and the Left can’t fix what they broke. We’re being punished with higher prices for everything. The cost of just getting by is skyrocketing and forcing families to live paycheck to paycheck. But we can fix it.

  • Stop spending what we don’t have and borrowing what we can’t pay back. 
  • Produce energy in America.
  • Build products in America.
  • Repeal the Biden tax hikes.
  • Fix the supply chain by reducing our dependence on China and other bad actors.
Standing Up To China

Troy Balderson caught China red-handed, attempting to undermine the American Steel industry. Now, more than ever, the enemies of freedom are testing America’s resolve.

  • Take away China’s biggest propaganda tool in America by forcing the sale of TikTok to an American company.
  • End our reliance on China for critical goods by bringing manufacturing back to the United States.
