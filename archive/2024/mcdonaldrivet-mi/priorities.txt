Where Kristen stands
My Priorities
Dear Neighbors,
Now more than ever, mid-Michigan families need elected leaders we trust to fight for us. I am a lifelong Michigander, raised in a house that my dad built himself. My husband and I raised six kids right here in mid-Michigan. And I have the honor of serving our communities in the State Senate. I spend my time in community, listening, and I know deeply the challenges and possibilities facing hard-working families across mid-Michigan.
I also have a record of working with leaders in both parties to deliver solutions for everyday problems impacting our community. I have taken on special interests and put more money in your pockets, protected our hard-earned freedoms, and supported law enforcement.
Now, I am running for Congress with the support of Congressman Kildee, to continue both of our work and deliver for you. Below are several of the priorities I plan to fight for, if you give me the chance to represent you in DC.
I hope to earn your vote by November 5. If you have questions, reach out to me and my team at
[email protected]
