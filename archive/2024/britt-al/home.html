About Katie

MOMMA ON A MISSION.

Katie Britt is fighting with hardworking parents to preserve the American Dream for future generations. Being the only Republican mom in the U.S. Senate with school-aged children, she sits at the forefront of some of the most important issues facing our nation’s families. Katie approaches any issue with strength, compassion, and an unwavering commitment to always fight for America.
