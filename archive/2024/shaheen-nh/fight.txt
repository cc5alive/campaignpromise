NEW TV AD: Senator Shaheen Makes a Difference for New Hampshire Veterans
August 3, 2020
In a new television ad, the Shaheen campaign highlights Jeanne Shaheen’s fight to make a difference for New Hampshire veterans: delivering health care options closer to home and outside the VA as well as tax credits for businesses to hire veterans, and cutting through red tape to ensure the men and women who fought for our country receive the benefits they deserve.
The new 30-second television ad features veterans from across New Hampshire, including veterans Jim Steiner of the U.S. Army Special Forces from Concord, Peter Vellis of the U.S. Navy from Bedford, Dan Proulx of the U.S. Marine Corps from Hudson, and Miriam Cahill-Yeaton of the U.S. Air Force from Epsom.
