NY-19 DEBATE DRAWS CLEAR CONTRAST ON ISSUES

 RILEY WILL FIGHT FOR UPSTATE, MOLINARO FIGHTS ONLY FOR HIMSELF

ALBANY, N.Y. —Tonight, Democratic Congressional candidate Josh Riley laid out his vision for the future of Upstate New York. One where women have the freedom to make decisions about their own bodies, where seniors have the ability to retire with dignity, and where working families, not big corporations, get the break they finally deserve.

“Josh Riley has a vision for Upstate New York that lifts people up and brings everyone along,” said Riley Campaign Manager Daniel Fleiss. “Marc Molinaro’s vision consists of racist lies, the 13 times he’s voted against a woman’s right to choose, and not much else.”
