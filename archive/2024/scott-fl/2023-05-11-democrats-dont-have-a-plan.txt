Washington, D.C. –
After weeks of fearmongering over the debt ceiling, Joe Biden is finally meeting with Speaker McCarthy. Why did it take Biden so long to start negotiating? Because Democrats
HATE
the debt ceiling. They don’t want any limits on how much of YOUR money they can borrow and spend.
Unfortunately for Joe Biden, Republicans control the House and passed a responsible debt limit bill that puts us on track to finally get our fiscal house in order. The House Republican bill also includes work requirements for all able-bodied adults. Republicans have a plan, Joe Biden and Chuck Schumer don’t.
Statement from Senator Rick Scott:
“Floridians are worried that they won’t be able to afford their next grocery bill. They’re stressing about when they will be able to comfortably retire. It would be irresponsible to raise the debt ceiling without addressing these issues through common-sense spending reforms. I’m going to do everything in my power to get the House Republican plan to the finish line. Floridians have to live within their means, so the federal government should too.”
