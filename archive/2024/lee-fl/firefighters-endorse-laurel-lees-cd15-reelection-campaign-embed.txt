Firefighters Endorse Laurel Lee’s CD15 Reelection Campaign
Republican candidate running for reelection to Florida Congressional District 15 Laurel Lee today announced that the Hillsborough County Fire Fighters, Pasco County Professional Firefighters, Polk County Professional Firefighters, Lakeland Professional Firefighters, Plant City Professional Firefighters and Temple Terrace Professional Firefighters have endorsed her campaign.
