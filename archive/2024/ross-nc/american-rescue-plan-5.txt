I was proud to help pass the American Rescue Plan that expanded access to safe childcare and early education opportunities, but this is just the first step we needed to make affordable childcare a reality in our state and country.
I look forward to heading back to Congress and putting this issue at the forefront of the fight to ensure we’re making smart investments for American families and the next generation.
