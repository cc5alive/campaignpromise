Great news

The U.S. Department of the Treasury
announced that, thanks to the American Rescue Plan, North Carolina is receiving more than $5.4 billion.
These funds provide support for North Carolina households, small businesses, impacted industries, essential workers, and the communities hit hardest by the pandemic.
