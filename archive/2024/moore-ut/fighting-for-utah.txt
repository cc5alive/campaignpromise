Fighting President Biden’s Failed Agenda for Utah Families
Achieving Results for Utahns
Medal of Honor Monument Act
As the House Republican freshman with the highest statistical legislative success rate, Blake Moore was proud to see his Medal of Honor Monument Act signed into law to honor our nation's heroes and tell their stories to inspire future generations of Americans.
Hill Air Force Base
Blake Moore delivered for Hill Air Force Base by passing into law their biggest priorities, including bringing 4,000 new jobs and six new buildings to Hill Air Force Base for our nuclear program; improving hiring of veterans; modernizing our depots; and allowing the Davis County community to assist with base infrastructure.
Debt & Deficit Task Force
Blake Moore created a Debt & Deficit Task Force in Ogden with local stakeholders and business leaders to shine a light on out-of-control spending, harness northern Utah's expertise to create solutions that will target spending, reverse the trajectory of our addiction to spending, and put America back on the right track.
Recreation and Public Lands
Blake Moore has pushed legislation to prevent wildfires; bring federal funding to Utah for a geological assessment of the Great Salt Lake; create free shooting ranges on public lands; and cut red tape for those facing housing challenges around our national parks. Rep. Moore’s Saline Lake Ecosystems in the Great Basin States Program Act is bringing federal funding to Utah for a geological assessment of the Great Salt Lake. His MAPLand Act was signed into law and will digitize federal land access records to allow hunters, bikers, hikers, and other land users to access public lands and open up access points to the public. Americans deserve to enjoy our public lands, and implementing principles of good governance into federal management practices will save money and time and simplify access for all who visit our great state.
Bringing Your Federal Tax Dollars Back to Utah
Blake Moore is focused on bringing your tax dollars back to your community. He is delivering on community project programming and infrastructure through close collaboration with northern Utah officials, securing millions in unprecedented funding for northern Utah. The projects that received funding will support our state's growing transit and multi-use transportation needs; support public safety missions; improve water infrastructure; support the Cache Valley Parkway; construct a bus maintenance and storage facility; invest in workforce development programs; support foster care students; enhance Utah's role as a hub for national security studies; expand Hill Air Force Base infrastructure; and more.
Strengthening National Security
Blake Moore is leading efforts to reshore critical supply chains and strengthen our military readiness and capabilities to counter the Chinese Communist Party and keep Americans safe. He is focused on supporting U.S. companies that are developing critical technologies, energy and mining products, and life-saving devices and medicines so our country can weather any crisis. He is proud to stand with Israel and has led multiple initiatives to support Israel as they respond to Hamas’s horrific terrorist attacks.
Fighting Back Against Woke Capitalism
Blake Moore believes in defending the free market and that Americans should be able to make their own financial decisions. He voted to block Biden’s ESG investment rule to prevent money managers and big banks from using Americans’ retirement savings to back liberal causes. He is also pushing back on an array of other bad Biden policies, from increasing IRS audits of middle-class families; to requiring banks to hand over information on small transactions and bank accounts to the federal government; to requiring students who sell their dorm room furniture using Venmo to fill out 1099-Ks and create digital trails for IRS agents; to forcing moms who are making small sales on Etsy to fill out new forms; to turning our financial system into the climate police. Blake is against Biden’s insistence on making life more expensive and harder on low and middle-income Americans who are just trying to live their lives.
Proudly Pro-Life
Blake Moore is fighting the Democrats' extreme policies that promote abortion up to birth. Blake earned an A+ rating on the Susan B. Anthony Pro-Life America National Pro-Life Scorecard, and he has cosponsored and voted for legislation that would support women in need who are facing unexpected pregnancies, including the Care for Her Act, Supporting Pregnant and Parenting Women and Families Act, Pregnant Students Rights Act, and Tax Relief for American Families and Workers Act. Blake believes that Congress, state and local government, and our communities must work hard to provide pregnant women and families with the support, economic freedom, and opportunities they need to choose life, better ensuring that every child has the chance to live and thrive.
Fighting the Biden Administration’s Failed Agenda for Utah Families
Fighting the Border Crisis
The southern border is a national security and humanitarian disaster caused by Democrats’ open border policies. Frustration is boiling over in Democrat-led cities, many of which are calling for Biden’s help as they struggle to deter thousands of migrants and provide shelter with over-stretched budgets. Meanwhile, the Biden Administration has overturned policies that helped deter illegal activity at the border. It has even considered implementing a “Remain in Texas” policy to replace the “Remain in Mexico” policy to deal with the influx of immigrants entering the country illegally. Blake Moore is fighting against the surging crisis, voting for H.R. 2 to secure the border and combat illegal immigration. Blake invited a border patrol agent to testify in front of the Oversight and Investigations Subcommittee on how President Biden's border policies are allowing drugs to flow into our communities. He is committed to keeping children safe from traffickers; strengthening our asylum system; and ending Biden’s catch and release approach. At the same time, Blake believes in providing temporary worker visas to the Utah businesses that are creating jobs for Americans in our local economy, and assisting refugees from Ukraine and other war zones.
Fighting to End the Biden Energy Ban
Blake Moore led House Republican efforts to end the Biden energy ban and lower dependence on foreign oil and gas. His legislation would rebuild American energy production, require the timely issuance of permits to drill, and lower skyrocketing gas prices so that fewer Utahns suffer from President Biden's executive overreach. He is also actively seeking ways that America can reform our broken permitting process and increase critical mineral production, which is increasingly important to maintaining our competitive edge over China and maintaining economic growth.
Fighting Reckless Spending
Blake Moore is fighting against Biden's tax-and-spend agenda and socialist efforts to kill jobs, lower wages, harm businesses, export jobs, and create massive new entitlement programs all while our current social programs rapidly approach fiscal cliffs. He opposed Biden’s harmful spending bills and helped drive legislation through the Ways and Means Committee to roll back Biden’s subsidies for the rich. He is supporting legislation to help small businesses and give tax breaks to low and middle-income families already suffering under increased regulation and inflation.
Fighting for Second Amendment Rights
Blake Moore introduced the Range Access Act requiring the Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management to ensure that each qualifying district has at least one designated public recreational shooting range that does not require a user fee. In addition, he is introducing legislation to expedite processing times at the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms (ATF) so law-abiding Americans can access suppressors in a more timely fashion; this bill will also supercharge conservation funding to promote healthy habitats and wildlife populations. Blake also fought back successfully against Biden's nomination of longtime gun control advocate David Chipman to head the ATF, forcing the White House to rescind Chipman's nomination. Blake Moore continues to fight for Utahns' Second Amendment rights by opposing Biden's efforts to discriminate against gun owners and the Second Amendment community, defending our right to carry, and expanding recreational shooting and hunting access.
Fighting for Transparency in Healthcare
Blake Moore is fighting for choice and transparency in our healthcare system to empower Utahns. He is cosponsoring legislation to lower health prices and prescription drug costs with competition, expand Health Savings Accounts, force big insurance companies and hospital systems to transparently report pricing, give patients better information about the quality of care that specific hospitals and providers deliver, and expand access to telehealth and cures.
Fighting Big Tech
Blake Moore is working closely with congressional Republicans to fight back against big tech censorship, protect our rights to privacy and free speech, and defend Americans' control over their data and how it is used and collected. Most importantly, Blake promotes efforts to keep kids safe from social media and hold big tech companies responsible for the harms they are causing to our children. Social media is exacerbating addiction and the youth mental health crisis, and parents must have full access and consent over any accounts used by minors.
Fighting Against Inflation
Blake Moore is fighting back against inflation and high prices by leading congressional forums on Biden-induced inflation and gas prices and voting to end subsidies that keep Americans out of the workforce, instruct committees to combat runaway inflation, and overturn regulations.
Fighting for Accountability with Biden's Afghanistan Withdrawal
Americans deserve to know what went wrong in Biden's disastrous handling of the Afghanistan withdrawal. Blake Moore introduced the Afghanistan Accountability Act to immediately identify the breakdown between Biden, the DOD, and the intelligence community that facilitated this crisis. The bill was incorporated into the National Defense Authorization Act, along with provisions to prohibit the DOD from providing any financial support to the Taliban, require plans to rescue Americans still in Afghanistan, and identify and recover U.S. military equipment left behind.
