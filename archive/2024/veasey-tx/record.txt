A few key accomplishments from
Marc's Record
Created monthly “Marc Means Business” initiative where he works at a constituent business for a day to better understand the needs of local business owners
Served as the lead plaintiff in Texas Voter ID lawsuit and as a named plaintiff in the Texas Redistricting lawsuit
Hosts annual job fair that connects thousands of district constituents with employers
Hosts regular “Congress on your Corner” events to connect with constituents and address issues important to our communities
Hosts annual Veterans Town Hall
Hosts annual Teen Summitt that seeks to place high school students with employment opportunities and provide professional development resources
Hosts small business forum to increase contracting for local businesses
Supported the American Airlines merger to save 20,000 local jobs
Opened and maintains bilingual Congressional offices
Co-Sponsor of comprehensive, bipartisan immigration reform
Authored legislation to increase education benefits for veterans
Secured $6 million for local agencies to help enroll families with the Affordable Care Act
Consistently fights to increase the minimum wage
Authored legislation to give a tax break to grandparents helping raise grandchildren
Supported a bill that would provide funding for post 9/11 veterans seeking to obtain a degree in a STEM field
Authored a resolution to declare September as “National Voting Rights Month”
Authored a bill that increases research and development funding at the Department of Energy (DOE) to help spur private sector innovation through awards for breakthrough technologies
