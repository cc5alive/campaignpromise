Schmitt for Senate Breaks Fundraising Record With $2 Million Haul — No Missouri Republican US Senate Candidate Has Raised More in First Two Quarters
Oct 16
|
News
St. Louis, MO –
Missouri US Senate candidate Eric Schmitt raised a massive $1,986,220.35 in the first two quarters of his campaign – more than any Missouri Republican US Senate candidate in modern history. Schmitt for Senate previously broke the fundraising record in the first quarter of the campaign and continued its record-breaking pace with its second quarter haul.
“Our America First campaign for US Senate has extraordinary momentum as we continue to fight against the Democrats’ radical agenda,” said
Missouri US Senate Candidate Eric Schmitt
. “Thank you, Missouri, for your unprecedented support in the first few months since launching this campaign. We remain best positioned to win the fight next November.”
