In Historic Fundraising Haul, Schmitt for Senate Announces A Record-Breaking $1.3M Raised Since Campaign Launch
Jul 20
|
News
St. Louis, MO
– Today, Missouri U.S. Senate Candidate Eric Schmitt announced a massive $1.3M raised, more than any candidate for U.S. Senate in Missouri history, since launching his campaign three months ago. A grand total of $1,333,176.45 was raised by 2,627 unique donors with 1,550 donors from across the state of Missouri. Schmitt for Senate has a total of over $1.1M cash on hand.
“Every dollar we raise brings us closer to keeping Missouri’s open senate seat red,” said Missouri U.S Senate Candidate Eric Schmitt. “Our historic, record-breaking fundraising haul from supporters across the state of Missouri shows unprecedented enthusiasm for our America First, Missouri First campaign. With extraordinary early support, we are best positioned to win the fight next November.”
