Meet Jennifer McClellan
Congresswoman Jennifer McClellan has a record of results, championing the passage of major progressive laws and fighting on behalf of her constituents in greater Richmond for the past 17 years. Now, Rep. McClellan is continuing to deliver for the people of Virginia, and making sure their voices are heard in Washington, D.C.
More About Jennifer McClellan
On the Issues
During her 16 years in the Virginia legislature, Congresswoman McClellan earned a reputation as a strong legislative champion for Virginians. She passed landmark laws to protect voting rights, safeguard abortion access, tackle climate change, rebuild crumbling schools, expand Obamacare in Virginia, protect workers rights, and reform Virginia’s criminal justice system.
