Why I'm Running
As one of eight children born to migrant farm workers in Laredo, I know first hand the importance of having a chance at the American Dream.
But I believe a chance isn’t enough.
We must have good schools, affordable healthcare, a strong economy, and offer a hand up so everyone can succeed. I’m running so I can continue fighting to keep the American Dream alive for every family in South Texas.
Henry Cuellar
