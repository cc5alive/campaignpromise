Proven Leadership. Tested Conservative. Dedicated Service. Endorsed by President Trump!
Mike Haridopolos is the Trump-endorsed conservative candidate for Congress. With a strong track record of proven leadership, Mike is committed to bringing tested integrity and dedicated service to Washington, D.C. to represent Brevard, Indian River, and Orange Counties. Mike will fight to stop illegal immigration, restore our economy, and protect our national security. Mike will steadfastly work to protect the Indian River Lagoon and support NASA and America's space program.
About Mike Haridopolos
Mike Haridopolos is an experienced leader, successful businessman, and lifelong conservative. He has served as a member of the Florida House of Representatives and State Senate, where he was elected President of the Senate from 2010-2012. Mike began his career teaching United States History and Political Science at what is now Eastern Florida State College and has always cared deeply about our country, our constitution, and our American values.
As a conservative lawmaker, Mike has pushed for greater government transparency, reduced property taxes, and transformed the Florida Senate into a more conservative policy body. After leaving office, he started MJH Consulting, a successful business and political consulting company. Mike previously appeared as a regular political analyst on Fox 35 Orlando. He lives in Indian Harbour Beach with his wife Dr. Stephanie Haridopolos and their three adult children.

Mike's Vision
Mike is focused on creating a stronger future for our community by:
Stopping Illegal Immigration
Addressing the catastrophic crisis at our southern border and securing our nation’s borders.
Fixing Our Economy
Making the American economy work for our families once again. Out-of-control inflation, higher taxes, and reckless spending are putting the American Dream out of reach for millions of Americans.
Reducing National Debt
Fighting reckless spending to preserve Social Security and Medicare for future generations. Mike faced a $4B budget shortfall in Florida and found common-sense solutions to balance the budget. He will do this again in Congress.
End Weaponization of Justice
Putting a stop to the misuse of the justice system. Mike will stand shoulder-to-shoulder key allies to end the weaponization of our justice system.
Protecting the Indian River Lagoon
Advancing common sense ideas to protect our environment and joining the Congressional Estuary Caucus.
Supporting Space Exploration
Promoting high-paying jobs and technological innovation through space exploration. Space is key to American technological innovation as well as our national security.
Tackling Crime & Public Safety
Backing law enforcement and defending Second Amendment rights. Mike will always “Back the Blue” and is grateful for the support of the Sheriffs of both Brevard and Indian River Counties.
Strengthening the Military & Veterans
Ensuring our military has the best technology and our veterans receive top-notch care. Our military preserves our freedom every day.
Implementing Term Limits
Supporting term limits to ensure citizens serve and return to the private sector. Florida is proof that term limits work.
