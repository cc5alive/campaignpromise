MEET CHUCK
Chuck Grassley lives on a family farm in New Hartford, Iowa, a short distance from the farmhouse where he was born in Butler County. He’s Iowa’s longest-serving U.S. Senator, and he remains firmly rooted in the state, bringing Midwestern common sense to the nation’s capital.

Results for Iowa – Finance Committee
Delivered financial lifelines to Iowa households, small businesses, hospitals, and farms with passage of 5 pandemic relief bills in 2020
Secured better trade agreement with Canada and Mexico signed by President Trump to boost exports for Iowa farmers, manufacturers, and financial service providers
Champions renewable homegrown energy, biofuels, wind, solar
Fights for access to rural health care, led expansion for telehealth coverage, and protections for rural hospitals and clinics
Delivered historic tax cuts for Iowa families, farms, and small businesses signed into law by President Trump
Passed legislation to help more Americans save for retirement and give employers more tools to invest in their workers’ post-employment financial security.
Delivered historic infrastructure investment for Iowa communities that are bringing tax dollars back to Iowa to improve roads, fix bridges, update locks and dams, build out rural broadband in underserved areas, upgrade airports, and improve sewage and water quality systems that will boost economic development and quality of life in local communities across Iowa.
