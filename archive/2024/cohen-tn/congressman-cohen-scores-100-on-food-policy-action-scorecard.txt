Congressman Cohen Scores 100% on Food Policy Action Scorecard
Posted
November 14, 2014
·
Add Comment
Congressman Steve Cohen has scored 100% on the Food Policy Action Scorecard for the 113th Congress
.
The National Food Policy Scorecard rates Members of Congress based on eighteen votes including:
Restoration of SNAP cuts (For)
Lifting restrictions on pesticides (Against)
A bill that prohibits the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers and the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) from implementing updates to the Clean Water Act.(Against)
Click here to read the full list of votes considered for this scorecard and how Congressman Cohen voted on them
.
From the Food Policy Action website:
Food Policy Action was established in 2012 through a collaboration of national food policy leaders in order to hold legislators accountable on votes that have an effect on food and farming. Our goal is to change the national dialogue on food policy by educating the public on how elected officials are voting on these issues. Through education and the National Food Policy Scorecard, more people will be armed with the information they need to vote with their forks and elect more food policy leaders across the country.
