Planned Parenthood Action Fund Endorses Congressman Steve Cohen
Posted
October 24, 2016
·
Add Comment
Congresssman Steve Cohen has earned the endorsement of Planned Parenthood Action Fund and its Planned Parenthood Federal PAC.
Planned Parenthood Action Fund is an independent, not-for-profit political organization engaging in legislative, educational, and electoral activity. The PP Federal PAC supports federal candidates who are pro-family planning and support access to safe and legal abortion.
More than 90% of the services provided by Planned Parenthood clinics is life-saving cancer screenings, birth control, prevention, testing and treatment of STDs, breast health services, pap tests, and sexual health education.
“You have been a strong supporter of women’s health and we know you will continue to be a dedicated advocate for Planned Parenthood Federation of America,” said Planned Parenthood Action Fund President Cecile Richards in the endorsement letter.
