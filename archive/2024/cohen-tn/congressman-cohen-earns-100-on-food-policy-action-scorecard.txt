Congressman Cohen Earns 100% on Food Policy Action Scorecard
Posted
October 18, 2016
·
Add Comment
Congressman Steve Cohen has earned a 100% rating from the Food Policy Action on the
2016 National Food Policy Scorecard, which can be read by clicking here
.
The Scorecard examines the voting records of all Members of the 114th Congress and rates them according to six key votes dealing with food safety, food access, subsidies, and nutrition, among many other measures.
“I am proud to receive the only perfect score in the Tennessee Congressional Delegation on the 114th Congress National Food Policy Scorecard. I will continue to support policies that reduce hunger and promote healthy living in the United States,” said Congressman Cohen.
