ENDORSEMENT: Family Policy Alliance of Kansas

September 12, 2020October 15, 2021

The Family Policy Alliance of Kansas has endorsed our Kansas Champion, Ron Estes.

It’s no surprise that one of the leading organizations in Kansas for family values and religious freedom would back Ron. He made a promise to defend life and uphold Kansas family values in Congress, and he’s worked to protect the unborn every step of the way because every life is sacred.

In Congress, Ron led the charge to end Planned Parenthood and other abortion providers’ ability to receive Title X family planning dollars, knowing that abortion is not health care. He has voted for pro-life legislation every chance he can and has urged Speaker Pelosi to allow a vote on the common sense Born Alive Abortion Survivors Protection Act. Ron also introduced the Down Syndrome Discrimination by Abortion Prohibition Act.

We need to make sure Ron returns to Washington to fight for our religious freedoms and the unborn – and the Family Policy Alliance of Kansas agrees.
