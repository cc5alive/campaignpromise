Meet Becca
Becca is the first woman and openly LGBTQ+ person to represent Vermont in Congress. As the daughter of working-class mother and immigrant father, Becca is deeply committed to defending American democracy and protecting vulnerable communities.
Now in Congress, Becca is a member of the House Judiciary Committee and a leader in the Progressive Caucus, where she is an outspoken defender of democracy, and civil rights. Becca believes that even in the face of immense challenges, we cannot back away from fighting for each other and building connections across differences. That is how we will save our democracy.
