What Jim’s opponent stands for

Every race is important this year. And right-wing Republicans are working to gain control everywhere, even here in Massachusetts.

We've already shared that Jim's opponent has already dumped $100,000 of his own money into this race and is willing to spend even more.

Jim represents you. He doesn't take money from corporate PACs and relies on grassroots dollars. Can you chip in now to support his reelection campaign?

Here's the thing, Jim's opponent has more than just a few downright-ridiculous beliefs. On some, he's gone full-extremist:

→ More than a year after the repugnant attempted insurrection on January 6, 2021, Jim's opponent was still pushing the mind-boggling big lie that Donald Trump won the election.

→ If you want to see who he'd model his service after, turn to the people he looks up to. Earlier this year, Jim's opponent re-tweeted Marjorie Taylor-Greene, as she attended a white supremacist rally.

Let's say it clearly to ensure there's no misunderstanding: Jim and his opponent have very different ideas about how we can better serve the people of Western and Central Massachusetts.
