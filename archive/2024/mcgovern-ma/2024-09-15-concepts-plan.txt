“Concepts of a plan”
Last week, Kamala Harris and Donald Trump took the stage in their first presidential debate. If you didn't watch, you missed a spectacular performance from the Vice President and a historically bad performance (even by his low standard) from Donald Trump.
Between jabs at Harris's ethnic background, more lies about his 2020 election loss, and a frankly shocking lie about immigrants eating pets, Donald Trump attempted to make his pitch to the American people for a second term. His plan (or in his words, "concepts of a plan")? A whole lot more of the same division, hatred, and incompetence which caused 81 million Americans to fire him 4 years ago.
Meanwhile, on the other side of the stage, Americans saw exactly the kind of new leadership we are looking for in Kamala Harris: poised, prepared, bold, and unwilling to back down in the defense of our nation's ideals.
I can tell you right now, that Democrats in Congress are eager to hit the ground running and do the important work of transforming the country. Will you chip in to our campaign to elect a strong, progressive majority in Congress?
