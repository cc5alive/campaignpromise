Anna Paulina Luna slams Harris for failed record on border, illegal immigration
RENO, Nev. – Florida Rep. Anna Paulina Luna
slammed
Vice President Kamala Harris for her failed record on the U.S. border crisis and role responsibility to combat illegal immigration while in office.
“Do you think that it’s acceptable that under Kamala Harris’ watch, 85,000 children have gone missing under the custody of the federal government?” Luna asked a crowd during a rally with Republican vice presidential nominee Sen. J.D. Vance, R-Ohio, in support of former President Donald Trump.
“Do you think that it’s acceptable that she’s ignored the rape, trafficking, torture of Hispanic women and children?” she said. “Do you think it’s okay she’s turned a blind eye to the fentanyl that’s destroyed our communities? Whether you are black, white or Hispanic, rich or poor, it is ruining our country.”
“And do you think that it is okay that she has turned a blind eye to our kids going off to college, young women to mothers raped and tortured by people who are coming here illegally, breaking the law and are known criminals,” she said.
Luna argued that as a “minority woman,” Harris “had a seat at the table and she did nothing,” which is the “worst thing you could possibly do.”
“J.D. Vance will be a solution when President Trump gets elected, and I say when because according to Kamala Harris’ polling, it’s not looking good for her team,” she said.
According to the
RealClearPolling
, Trump has a four point lead over Harris in the swing state of Nevada.
