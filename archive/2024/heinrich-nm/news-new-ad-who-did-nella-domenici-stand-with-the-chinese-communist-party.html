NEW AD: “Who Did Nella Domenici Stand With? The Chinese Communist Party.”

Friday, August 2, 2024

ALBUQUERQUE, NM — Senator Martin Heinrich’s campaign today released a new ad, titled “Not Ours,” that highlights how Bridgewater Associates, where Nella Domenici served as Chief Financial Officer, invested in Chinese military companies including some “that produce fighter jets, bombers, planes equipped to jam enemy radars, and the country’s first domestically built aircraft carrier,” and expanded its deep ties with the Chinese Communist Party.

“New Mexicans deserve to know where their leaders stand—and it’s clear Nella Domenici was more interested in padding her pockets than protecting our national security,” said Brad Elkins, campaign manager. “While Martin Heinrich has been working tirelessly to keep our country safe and invest in our communities, Nella was profiting off investments that directly undermine America’s security interests.”

Nella Domenici was one of six people responsible for overseeing and managing all aspects of Bridgewater’s business operations. Domenici called her tenure at Bridgewater—the world’s largest hedge fund—the “pinnacle” of her career.

SCRIPT:

Given the choice…who did Nella Domenici stand with?
The Chinese Communist Party,
A million times over.
Nella served as CFO of hedge fund Bridgewater,
Which made millions by pouring money into companies building China’s fighter jets.
Bombers and aircraft carriers,
Weapons of war that can be turned on America.
Our national security traded for her personal gain.
Nella Domenici already chose a side.
And it wasn’t ours.

The ad will air statewide in New Mexico. Watch “Not Ours” here.
