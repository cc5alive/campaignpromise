Accomplishments

Workforce Development

The American dream does not require a 4-year degree and a mountain of student loan debt. There are many different paths to take in the pursuit of happiness and Max is working to ensure every American can pursue the path that’s right for them. Max has made it his mission to expand workforce development and career and technical education opportunities that create prosperity for individuals & build a stronger future for America.

  • As promised, Max donated 25% of his congressional salary back to the Northeast Ohio community. He donated $43,500 to Medina County Career to fund scholarships for career and technical education students.
  • Co-sponsored the National Apprenticeship Act (H.R. 2851) to create nearly one million new registered apprenticeships, youth apprenticeships, and pre-apprenticeship opportunities over the next five years.
  • Co-sponsored the Bipartisan Workforce Pell Act (H.R. 6585) to expand Pell Grants for students to enroll in high-quality, short-term training programs in addition to traditional college degree programs.
  • Co-sponsored the Aviation Workforce Development Act (H.R. 1818) to allow 529 distributions to pay education-related expenses required for certain aviation maintenance and commercial pilot courses.
  • Co-sponsored the Training and Nutrition Stability Act (H.R. 3087) to incentivize federal benefit recipients to pursue employment opportunities and workforce training.
  • Co-sponsored the Supporting Apprenticeship Colleges Act of 2023 (H.R. 3179) to direct the Department of Education to award grants to institutions of higher education that sponsor construction and manufacturing-oriented registered apprenticeship programs.
Education

Political agendas should not be a higher priority than educating Ohioans. We must reject critical race theory and sexual indoctrination at our schools. School choice will allow parents to remove their kids from schools that fail them or attempt to indoctrinate them. Kids should be learning about American civics and skills that will allow them to live a worthwhile life.

  • Co-sponsored the Parents Bill of Rights (H.R. 5) to ensure transparency for parents in their children’s education.
  • Introduced the Ensuring Military Recruitment Access Act (H.R. 4522) to ensure recruiters for our Armed Forces have access to secondary school campuses to share opportunities with students.
  • Co-sponsored the Educational Choice for Children Act (H.R. 531)  to incentivize individuals and corporations to provide scholarships to students.
  • Co-sponsored the Protect Our Children’s Schools Act (H.R. 2476) to require leftover COVID-19 funds to be made available to local educational agencies to keep schools safe.
  • Co-sponsored the School Resource Officer Compensation Act (H.R. 2478) to incentivize school districts to hire retired law enforcement officers to serve as School Resource Officers.
  • Co-sponsored the Securing Our Schools Act (H.R. 2479) to enhance school security by doubling school resource officer staff, hiring more school-based mental health counselors, and expanding the nonprofit security grant program.
  • Co-sponsored the Protect Children’s Innocence Act (H.R. 1399) to prohibit gender-affirming care on minors.
Foreign Policy

Joe Biden’s foreign policy failures have allowed other nations to fill the power vacuum. The botched withdrawal in Afghanistan was the first domino to fall. Russian war in Ukraine, a nuclear Iran, North Korea firing missiles again, a deteriorating relationship with China, and the deadliest day for the Jewish people since the Holocaust have all followed. We need an America-first foreign policy that allows us to lead where we need to, protect our interests, and support our allies.

  • Co-sponsored the Fight CRIME Act (H.R. 3152) to impose sanctions on countries, individuals, and other entities that attempt to support Iranian missiles and related goods and technology, including materials and equipment.
  • Cosponsored the SHIP Act (H.R. 3774) to require the President to impose sanctions against foreign persons who knowingly deal in petroleum products originating in Iran.
  • Co-sponsored the U.S.-Israel Partnership and Abraham Accords Enhancement Act of 2023 (H.R. 3792) to strengthen cooperation between the United States and Israel and expand and enhance the Abraham Accords.
  • Co-sponsored the Securing Our Airspace from Reconnaissance Act of 2023 (H.R. 3974) to prohibit the use of Federal funds for unmanned aircraft systems from foreign entities.
  • Cosponsored the PASS Act (H.R. 683) to prohibit foreign adversaries like China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea from purchasing U.S. farmland and agricultural companies.

Americans are tired of elected officials going to Washington to cash in. Many politicians use insider information and their positions to gain personal wealth and status. Agriculture

One of the largest industries in Ohio is agriculture. Farmers feed us and drive the American economy. Farmers are also some of the biggest victims of the Biden-Inflation disaster. Rising energy costs and input costs across the board are crushing our farmers and Max will be a voice in Washington working to get Ohio’s farmers the support they need to weather this storm.

  • Convenes an Agricultural Advisory Council to learn about the priorities of Northeast Ohio’s agricultural community directly from industry experts.
  • Introduced the Farm to Fly Act of 2023 (H.R. 6271) to create new markets for American agricultural products and strengthen domestic energy resources.
  • Introduced the Farm to Fuselage Act (H.R. 5235) to update the Farm Bill to expand the role of U.S. agriculture in building our nation’s fuel supply.
Energy

Americans are already suffering from the impacts of inflation. The Biden administration has piled on by raising the costs of energy with policies that put China, the Middle East, and Russia first by cutting American energy production off at the knees. The time has come to re-commit to American energy independence and make us safer, provide cheaper energy, and put Americans back to work.

  • Helped pass the Lower Energy Costs Act (H.R. 1) to restore American energy independence by increasing domestic energy production, reversing anti-energy policies by the Biden Administration, and boosting the production and processing of critical minerals.
  • Co-sponsored the Promoting Domestic Energy Production Act (H.R. 5073) to allow American energy companies to deduct costs, including labor and safety, associated with oil and gas exploration.
  • Introduced the Farm to Fly Act of 2023 (H.R. 6271) to create new markets for American agricultural products and strengthen domestic energy resources.
Securing our southern border and fighting the scourge of fentanyl

The lawlessness at the southern border has led to illicit drugs on our streets, dangerous criminals getting rich, and human trafficking of innocent victims. Mass migration places strains on services across America. Criminals who should have never been able to enter our country do so freely. The radical position of open borders has made all of us less safe as dangerous drugs like fentanyl kill Americans right here in Ohio’s 7th Congressional District.

  • Helped pass the Secure the Border Act (H.R. 2) to take meaningful action to end the crisis at the US-Mexico border such as resuming construction on the border wall, providing for an additional 22,000 Border Patrol agents, and bolstering local law enforcement jurisdiction along the southern border.
  • Helped pass the HALT Fentanyl Act (H.R. 467) to permanently place fentanyl-related substances in the Schedule 1 category to designate it as having no medical use and a high potential for abuse.
  • Co-sponsored the STOP Fentanyl Act (H.R. 335) to create enhanced prison terms for importing or exporting fentanyl along the U.S.-Mexico border.
  • Co-sponsored the TRANQ Research Act (H.R. 1734) to direct the National Institute of Standards and Technology to research fentanyl additives such as TRANQ.
  • Co-sponsored the SOS Act of 2023 (H.R. 3666) to direct the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate illicit fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction.

We need to do all we can to make America the best place on the planet to create a job. Job creators of all sizes are attacked for their success.
