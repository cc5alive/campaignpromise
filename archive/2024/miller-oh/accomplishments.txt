Workforce Development
The American dream does not require a 4-year degree and a mountain of student loan debt. There are many different paths to take in the pursuit of happiness and Max is working to ensure every American can pursue the path that’s right for them. Max has made it his mission to expand workforce development and career and technical education opportunities that create prosperity for individuals & build a stronger future for America.
As promised, Max donated 25% of his congressional salary back to the Northeast Ohio community.
He donated $43,500 to Medina County Career to fund scholarships for career and technical education students.
Co-sponsored the National Apprenticeship Act (H.R. 2851)
to create nearly one million new registered apprenticeships, youth apprenticeships, and pre-apprenticeship opportunities over the next five years.
Co-sponsored the Bipartisan Workforce Pell Act (H.R. 6585)
to expand Pell Grants for students to enroll in high-quality, short-term training programs in addition to traditional college degree programs.
Co-sponsored the Aviation Workforce Development Act (H.R. 1818)
to allow 529 distributions to pay education-related expenses required for certain aviation maintenance and commercial pilot courses.
Co-sponsored the Training and Nutrition Stability Act (H.R. 3087)
to incentivize federal benefit recipients to pursue employment opportunities and workforce training.
Co-sponsored the Supporting Apprenticeship Colleges Act of 2023 (H.R. 3179)
to direct the Department of Education to award grants to institutions of higher education that sponsor construction and manufacturing-oriented registered apprenticeship programs.

Russian war in Ukraine, a nuclear Iran, North Korea firing missiles again, a deteriorating relationship with China, and the deadliest day for the Jewish people since the Holocaust have all followed. Cosponsored the SHIP Act (H.R. 3774)

Co-sponsored the U.S.-Israel Partnership and Abraham Accords Enhancement Act of 2023 (H.R. 3792)
to strengthen cooperation between the United States and Israel and expand and enhance the Abraham Accords.
Co-sponsored the Securing Our Airspace from Reconnaissance Act of 2023 (H.R. 3974)
to prohibit the use of Federal funds for unmanned aircraft systems from foreign entities.
Cosponsored the PASS Act (H.R. 683)
to prohibit foreign adversaries like China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea from purchasing U.S. farmland and agricultural companies.

Securing our southern border and fighting the scourge of fentanyl
The lawlessness at the southern border has led to illicit drugs on our streets, dangerous criminals getting rich, and human trafficking of innocent victims. Mass migration places strains on services across America. Criminals who should have never been able to enter our country do so freely. The radical position of open borders has made all of us less safe as dangerous drugs like fentanyl kill Americans right here in Ohio’s 7th Congressional District.
Helped pass the Secure the Border Act (H.R. 2)
to take meaningful action to end the crisis at the US-Mexico border such as resuming construction on the border wall, providing for an additional 22,000 Border Patrol agents, and bolstering local law enforcement jurisdiction along the southern border.
Helped pass the HALT Fentanyl Act (H.R. 467)
to permanently place fentanyl-related substances in the Schedule 1 category to designate it as having no medical use and a high potential for abuse.
Co-sponsored the STOP Fentanyl Act (H.R. 335)

to direct the National Institute of Standards and Technology to research fentanyl additives such as TRANQ.
Co-sponsored the SOS Act of 2023 (H.R. 3666)
to direct the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate illicit fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction.
