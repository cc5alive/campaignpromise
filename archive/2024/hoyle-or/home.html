Val Hoyle for Congress

Val Hoyle’s record of standing up for Oregon’s hardworking families and small businesses stems from a lifetime of experience. From her union roots, to her leadership in the state legislature and her tenure as Labor Commissioner, she always puts working people first.

Val Always Fights for Oregon

Reproductive Healthcare

Val has helped expand access to reproductive healthcare. In Congress, she’ll fight against anti-abortion extremists.

Working Families

Val helped pass paid sick leave – and is a champion of apprenticeship programs.

Our Environment

Val expanded Oregon’s Clean Fuels program to reduce carbon emissions. In Congress, she’ll continue working to transition us to a clean energy future.

Oregon Workers

Val increased the state’s minimum wage.

Voting Rights

Val expanded ballot access through Oregon’s groundbreaking “Motor Voter” law.

Gun Safety

Val sponsored and passed a law requiring universal background checks for all firearm sales.
