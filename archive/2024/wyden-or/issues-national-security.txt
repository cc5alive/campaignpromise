America’s Place in the World
Ron believes that America must lead the world by building consensus, promoting human rights, and tackling the climate crisis.
In trade, America can create prosperity at home by leveling the playing field for American businesses and workers so that we can make things here and sell them around the world.
Ron was one of just 23 U.S. Senators to vote against the war in Iraq and has worked for years to resolve conflicts and bring peace to the Middle East.
Ron believes America plays an important role and has a moral obligation to welcome those fleeing persecution from other countries and seeking refuge in the United States. Immigrants strengthen our nation and Ron supports policies that would end the atrocities of separating families and other inhumane actions taken by the White House.
