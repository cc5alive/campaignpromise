Why I’m running
I love Alaska and am proud to call this special place my home. I am honored to be your voice in the U.S. Senate. With your support and trust, we have accomplished a lot of great things for our great state. I am running to continue the important work of growing our economy, strengthening our Alaska-based military and protecting our people and the natural beauty of our state.
