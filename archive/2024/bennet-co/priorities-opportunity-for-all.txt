Expand Opportunity For All
Michael's Vision
Build Economic Opportunity in Colorado:
Create opportunities for Coloradans to get ahead by investing deeply in career and technical education, increasing access to apprenticeship programs, and prioritizing broadband access particularly in the more rural and underserved parts of the state.
Raise the Minimum Wage:
Raise the federal minimum wage to $15. Make sure that everyone who works full-time can live with dignity. Build an economy that creates opportunity and security for everyone.
Eliminate the Gender Pay Gap:
Create equity in pay for women, and particularly women of color, by increasing transparency and giving women more power in salary negotiations, ending pay discrimination, and giving women the resources needed to negotiate higher salaries and better benefits.
