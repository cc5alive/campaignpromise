Tackle Generational Challenges
Michael's Vision
Fight Climate Change Head On and Protect Colorado Lands:
Pass the CORE Act to protect 400,000 acres of Colorado public lands; continue investing in clean energy to create jobs and opportunities for Coloradans for years to come; addressing water shortages and droughts plaguing Colorado farmers and ranchers; and protect Colorado forests, fields, and homes from the wildfires that have become so destructive in recent years.
End the National Epidemic of Gun Violence:
Protect Coloradans from senseless gun violence and death by passing universal background checks; closing the gun show loophole; limiting the size of magazines; and keeping weapons of war off our streets.
Reform Campaign Finance Laws and Our Broken Political System:
Take the influence of money out of politics and put the power back with the American people so we can make progress on these challenges, including banning Members of Congress from ever becoming lobbyists, ending partisan gerrymandering, and ensuring every eligible voter has access to vote.
Pursue A Bold, Humane Immigration System:
Create a pathway to citizenship for DREAMers, prioritize family reunification, and keep families together.
