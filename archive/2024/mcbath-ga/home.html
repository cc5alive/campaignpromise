ON THE ISSUES

LGBTQ Rights

The fight for equality and justice must continue with protections for our LGBTQ+ communities. Republicans across the nation are launching unconscionable discriminatory acts against the LGBTQ+ community, which is why we must do all we can to pass non-discriminatory legislation…Read more.

Women's Rights

As a two-time breast cancer survivor, Congresswoman McBath understands the importance of affordable and quality healthcare. As a mom who struggled to get pregnant, she opposed the G.O.P. plan to take away healthcare from thousands of Georgians by defunding Planned Parenthood…Read more.

Infrastructure

President Biden has delivered a once-in-a-generation investment in our nation’s aging infrastructure. Congresswoman McBath has been an unwavering supporter of the President because our communities deserve access to safe drinking water, high-speed internet, and a cleaner environment….Read more.

Money in Politics

Congresswoman McBath strongly disagrees with the Citizens United decision. Corporate interests have had a corrosive effect on our democracy, and she will support any legislation to increase transparency of our campaign finance system… Read more.
