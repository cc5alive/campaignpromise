NEW AD: Juan Ciscomani Is Fighting To Make DC Listen
Jun 24
Written By
Blake Wilson
TUCSON –
Republican Congressman Juan Ciscomani launched a new ad today, highlighting his efforts to wake up politicians in Washington to the challenges at the border and focus on real solutions.
Since being elected to Congress in 2022, Juan has been a vocal advocate for Arizona and has successfully passed bipartisan legislation to
deal with the impact of the border crisis
.
“The immigration crisis at our border is real, and we need people in Washington who are going to actually listen to the Arizonans facing this every day,” Juan said. “For too long, we’ve had politicians in Washington ignore this problem or use it as a political issue. Arizonans want solutions, and that’s my focus.”
Watch the ad here:
https://youtu.be/PZ9BpmyeTms
Script:
Drugs. Organized crime. Human trafficking.
Here in Arizona – Washington’s failures are truly a matter of life and death.
I’m Juan Ciscomani, and I’m fighting everyday to make the politicians in D.C. wake up and listen to us.
More resources for border patrol. Tougher penalties on fentanyl dealers. And restarting border wall construction.
I approved this message because let's face it: President Biden’s open border policies have been a complete disaster.
To learn more about Congressman Ciscomani's policy priorities, community outreach initiatives, and contact information, please visit:
www.JuanCiscomani.com
.
