Meet Jacky

U.S. Senator Jacky Rosen is a fighter for hardworking families, an independent voice standing up for Nevada, and a bipartisan problem solver who knows how to bring people together and work across party lines to get results.

Jacky is running for reelection to continue delivering for Nevadans. She is focused on getting things done and not playing the same old Washington games, which is why she’s ranked as one of the most bipartisan, independent, and effective Senators. In the Senate, she has never hesitated to take on powerful special interests to bring down costs for hardworking Nevada families. Jacky puts Nevada first and will work with anyone – Republican, Democrat, or Independent – to do what’s right for our state.
