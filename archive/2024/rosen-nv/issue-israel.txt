On the Issues
Combating Antisemitism and Standing with Israel
As a former synagogue president and the only Jewish woman in the Senate, Jacky is the leading voice in Congress on the urgent need to combat rising antisemitism. She co-founded and co-chairs the Senate Bipartisan Task Force for Combating Antisemitism and has written and passed bipartisan legislation to fund Holocaust education in our schools and increase security resources for synagogues and Jewish community centers. She also successfully pushed the White House to create the first-ever U.S. National Strategy to Counter Antisemitism.
Jacky firmly believes in the importance of a strong U.S.-Israel relationship and has consistently advocated for bipartisan support and sustained assistance for Israel to defend itself against threats. In the wake of Hamas’s deadly October 7 terrorist attack on Israel, Jacky has pushed her colleagues and the Administration to provide Israel the resources and support it needs to defend its people, free the hostages, and defeat Hamas. She believes the U.S. must support Israel as it works to defeat Hamas while also allowing for more humanitarian aid for civilians in Gaza and doing everything possible to prevent the loss of innocent lives.
Jacky believes in a two-state solution, with a secure, democratic, and Jewish State of Israel living side-by-side with a peaceful Palestinian state. She also understands that Hamas is an impediment to peace and poses a threat to both Israelis and Palestinians.
Jacky co-founded the bipartisan Senate Abraham Accords Caucus, and she led a historic, bipartisan delegation visit to the Middle East in January 2023 encouraging the expansion of positive cooperation between Israel and Arab nations.
