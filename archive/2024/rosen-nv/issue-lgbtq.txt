On the Issues
Fighting for Equality
Nevada is home to a vibrant LGBTQ+ community, and Jacky has always been a committed ally in the fight for equality. She helped pass historic, bipartisan legislation to protect marriage equality at the federal level. At a time when the LGBTQ+ community is under unprecedented attack, Jacky believes more needs to be done to protect Nevadans – regardless of who they are or who they love. That’s why she helped introduce the Equality Act to explicitly prohibit discrimination on the basis of sexual orientation and gender identity in core areas of everyday life – including education, employment, and housing. Every person deserves equal protection under the law.
