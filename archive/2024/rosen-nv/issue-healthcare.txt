On the Issues
Expanding Access to Health Care
Jacky will always put Nevada’s families ahead of the politicians and special interests in Washington who want to threaten access to quality, affordable health care. She is fighting every single day to lower the cost of health care and prescription drugs, address the physicians and nurses shortage affecting Nevada, and expand access to telemedicine and rural health care.
In the Senate, Jacky helped pass a new law to lower costs for Nevadans by capping the price of insulin at $35 for seniors and empowering Medicare to negotiate lower drug prices, and she passed her bipartisan legislation to expand mobile health clinics to increase access to health care for underserved areas in Nevada. Jacky is also pushing to ensure rural Nevadans have access to the reliable, affordable health care they deserve by introducing bipartisan legislation that would incentivize medical providers to work in Nevada’s underserved communities. She is working to lower costs for family caregivers and standing strong to protect Medicare benefits for our seniors.
