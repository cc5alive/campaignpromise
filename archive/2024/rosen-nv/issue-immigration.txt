On the Issues
Fixing Our Broken Immigration System and Securing the Border
Jacky strongly believes in the need for comprehensive, bipartisan immigration reform that secures our borders, protects DACA and TPS recipients, provides pathways to citizenship, fixes our broken immigration system, and overhauls our backlogged asylum process.
Jacky has voted to deliver billions of dollars in resources to​ improve U.S.-Mexico border secur​i​ty and helped pass bipartisan laws to stop the flow of deadly fentanyl at our southern border. This year, she worked with both parties to support a bipartisan border security deal that would make historic investments to secure the border, including funding new security and screening technology, hiring more Border Patrol agents, and increasing resources for Customs and Border Protection officers. Despite the National Border Patrol Council and the acting chief of the U.S. Customs and Border Patrol endorsing the bipartisan plan, extreme Republicans in Congress blocked it because they would rather use this issue for political gain than take real action to address a growing crisis.
Passing immigration reform will improve public safety, grow our workforce, and strengthen Nevada’s economy – which is why Jacky has continued to push her colleagues on both sides of the aisle to come to the negotiating table and find common ground. Nevada’s immigrant families are part of the fabric of our communities, and Jacky won’t stop fighting to find a solution that provides permanent relief for the thousands of Dreamers and TPS recipients who call Nevada home.
