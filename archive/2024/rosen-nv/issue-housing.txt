On the Issues
Making Housing More Affordable
Let’s face it; the sky-rocketing costs of buying a house or affording rent have gotten out of control. These increasing prices are putting an untenable strain on hardworking Nevadans, and Jacky is taking action to solve the problem.
Jacky helped secure $500 million for the state of Nevada to create more housing options for hardworking families through the American Rescue Plan, including millions of dollars for rural areas.
With a surge in institutional investors buying up single-family homes, Jacky is working to prevent price gouging in the housing market and crack down on these corporate investors driving up housing costs across the state. She helped introduce a new plan to lower costs and cut taxes for first-time homebuyers who need down payment assistance. And as Nevada’s biggest cities continue to grow, Jacky is leading ongoing efforts to open up thousands of acres of federally owned land in Washoe County to build more housing that Nevadans can afford.
