On the Issues
Defending Democracy
Voting rights are the bedrock of our democracy, and Jacky will always stand up against efforts to restrict the fundamental right of every eligible American to make their voice heard at the ballot box. She has been a vocal advocate for the bipartisan John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act, which would restore and strengthen the landmark Voting Rights Act to combat voter suppression. She also helped introduce the Freedom to Vote Act, comprehensive legislation to protect the right to vote by making it easier for eligible voters to cast a ballot, increasing election security, and strengthening campaign finance laws to boost transparency.
Following the deadly January 6 insurrection and dangerous efforts to overturn the will of Nevada voters, Jacky has worked to protect our democracy against these unprecedented attacks fueled by false conspiracy theories. In 2022, Jacky helped pass a bipartisan plan to reform the system of counting electoral votes to preserve the will of the people and stop future attempts to overturn our elections.
