On the Issues

Delivering Results

In Congress, Nikki is working everyday to deliver for working families in Central and Southern Illinois. Nikki was rated one of the most bipartisan freshman Member of Congress

Nikki has brought over $288 million in federal grants back to Illinois’s 13th Congressional District. Nikki’s first piece of legislation was the LEAP Act to incentivize small businesses to hire directly from apprenticeship programs.

"At the heart of her work, is working with and forming relationships with fellow Democrats and House Republicans…

- The State Journal-Register (4/12/23)
