Stopping Illegal Immigration

As a border state, illegal immigration disproportionately affects our state. In Congress, David Schweikert has always fought to secure Arizona’s border and stop illegal immigration. David was a strong supporter of President Trump’s border wall. He has also worked tirelessly to stop left-wing proposals to open our border and give amnesty to illegal immigrants.
