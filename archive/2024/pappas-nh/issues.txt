On the Issues — Chris Pappas for Congress
On the Issues
CREATING AN ECONOMY THAT WORKS FOR all
Lowering costs for working families remains my top priority in Congress, and I’ll keep fighting to bring down prices and make living more affordable from health care and child care to groceries and energy. We’ve made important progress –
Allowing Medicare to negotiate lower prescription drug prices and capping out-of-pocket costs for seniors,
Capping the cost of insulin at $35,
Lowering the cost of health insurance on New Hampshire’s ACA exchange,
Increasing benefits for disabled veterans and their families to keep up with the cost of living,
Lowering energy costs through tax credits for households and small businesses,
Securing supply chains and investing in critical infrastructure.
I’ll continue putting New Hampshire families first — prioritizing people over politics or profit — and working to create an economy that works for everyone.
I’m fighting for bipartisan, commonsense solutions to lower taxes and cut costs for small businesses and working families. We must expand the Child Tax Credit to put more money back in the pockets of hard-working families and tackle the affordable housing crisis that New Hampshire is facing. And as we work to lower the deficit and ensure the biggest corporations pay their fair share, I won’t support any legislation that increases taxes on those making less than $400,000 a year while working to ensure our small businesses have the incentives they need to succeed.
Making housing more affordable
New Hampshire’s need for affordable housing grows by the day, and too many working families in our state are struggling to make ends meet. I’ve helped secure more than $40 million in federal funding to help strengthen our stock of affordable housing, and I am fighting to expand the Low Income Housing Tax Credit which will allow for more development. I will continue to ensure Washington provides the help our communities need to modernize zoning and the financial tools that will make more projects viable. It's going to take all of us working together to resolve this issue. I remain committed to working with local leaders, advocates, and partners; listening to their concerns; and delivering federal resources that can help create more affordable housing in every corner of our state.
protecting reproductive rights
Every woman deserves the right to make her own decisions about her body, her family, and her future. The overturning of
Roe v. Wade
and the extreme attacks on reproductive health care are threatening the safety and freedoms for women that should be guaranteed.
Granite Staters have made it clear that they do not want politicians involved in their personal health care decisions. I’ll keep fighting to restore reproductive freedom and stop extremists from turning back the clock on women’s rights.
STanding up for our veterans
As a member of the Veterans’ Affairs Committee, I’ve made advocating for veterans and their families a central priority of my time in Congress, and I’ll always fight to ensure our veterans receive the health care they need and the benefits they’ve earned.
I worked across the aisle to pass the PACT Act which is delivering long overdue care for veterans exposed to toxic substances during their service. I fought to increase veterans’ benefits to keep up with the cost of living, helped maintain and expand critical VA services in New Hampshire, and proposed bipartisan legislation to improve benefits for veterans’ family members and survivors. As long as I have this job, veterans will have a staunch ally in Congress.
creating safer communities
Granite Staters and families across the nation deserve to live in safe communities, and I’ll keep working to make that a reality. That’s why I’ve fought to secure millions of dollars in funding to support the work of police, fire, and other emergency personnel in New Hampshire. I’ve written legislation to invest in our public safety infrastructure and am pushing for a bill to protect the retirement savings of public employees. My bill to ensure law enforcement can prevent deadly opioids from coming into our communities has become law, and I’ll keep fighting for resources to expand treatment and recovery and address New Hampshire’s addiction epidemic.
I’ll continue to fight for commonsense reforms to protect our communities from gun violence. I helped pass bipartisan legislation that closed loopholes in our gun laws, implemented basic safety policies like background checks, and made significant investments in community safety that are saving lives.
I’ve earned the endorsement of the Professional Fire Fighters of New Hampshire and leading gun violence prevention organizations Giffords, Brady PAC and Everytown for Gun Safety, for my continued work to make our state and country safer.
fixing our immigration system
Throughout my time in Congress, I have pushed for greater funding that will provide security at our border and legislation that will fix our broken immigration system. I have consistently called for both parties to come together to pass commonsense border legislation. I will continue to advocate for further action to ensure that our Southern and Northern borders are secure, deploy technology and crack down on the trafficking of deadly substances like fentanyl, and address the deficiencies in our laws.
We need a system of legal immigration that supports our communities and economic future and adheres to our American values. It’s well past time to put aside politics and focus on bringing Republicans and Democrats together to get this done for the American people, and I remain committed to working across the aisle to do just that.
protecting our environment
I am committed to addressing the threat of climate change, making our communities more resilient and sustainable, and safeguarding New Hampshire’s natural resources for future generations. That’s why I fought to pass historic legislation that will drastically lower emissions and has provided funding to help New Hampshire communities tackle the climate crisis and modernize our infrastructure.
We can create a more sustainable future that protects what’s special about New Hampshire, creates good-paying jobs, and boosts our economy. I’ll keep fighting for innovative solutions to the climate crisis that keep the priorities of our communities and families at the forefront.
