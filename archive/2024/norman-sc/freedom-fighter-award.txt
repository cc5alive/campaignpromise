Freedom Fighter Award
One of the most sought-after awards for Members of Congress who truly care about lower taxes and less government is called the Freedom Fighter Award.
Of the approximately 535 Members in the House and Senate, only 38 received this award for their work last year. I am honored to be among them. In fact, it’s my second consecutive year receiving this award from FreedomWorks.
Friends, our government has a necessary and important role in our society, especially during a time like we’re in now. Yet left unchecked, the natural tendency of the federal government is to infringe on our liberties and place unsustainable debt on the shoulders of our children & grandchildren. Nowhere is that more evident than in the platform of today’s Democratic party.
As long as I’m in Congress, I’ll do everything I can to promote the values this award represents: limited government and lower taxes.
