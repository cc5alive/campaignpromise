Aloha!
I’m Congressman Ed Case, asking for your kōkua to continue my service to our Hawai‘i and country in our U.S. Congress.
In these very difficult times, we don’t need more division and inaction. We do need strong, steady leadership focused on real solutions to real challenges.
I was born and raised in Hilo with deep roots throughout Hawai‘i. I’ve had a full career in law and business, and have a great family.
I served eight years in our Hawai‘i Legislature and now ten years on Capitol Hill. I hold key positions of responsibility like our Appropriations Committee which funds our federal government. I have fought successfully for key domestic and foreign policy initiatives, and have addressed crises for our country and our Hawai‘i like COVID-19, the Red Hill fuel spill, and Maui wildfires.
Throughout it all, I have believed that we can and must find a better way forward, together.
No yelling and screaming. Just facing our challenges honestly and openly – working hard and solving our problems.
That’s what it takes. That’s what I’ve done. That’s what I continue to offer.
I deeply appreciate your consideration and ask for your support.
Mahalo nui loa,
