America is at a crossroads. What once seemed unfathomable, is suddenly our reality.
Our southern border is being overwhelmed with millions entering illegally from around the world, while fentanyl pours into our communities and now ranks as the #1 killer of Americans aged 18-45.
Meanwhile, the Biden-Harris Administration does nothing.
Not only is China our top economic adversary, they are sending spy balloons over our heartland, buying our farmland, building our factories, and corrupting our children.
Meanwhile, the Biden-Harris Administration stands by as we give them our tax dollars.
Violent crime is gripping our cities and spilling into our suburbs. Even Lansing ranks among the most violent cities in the country.
Meanwhile, the Biden-Harris Administration attacks our police.
Our federal debt is now $34 TRILLION dollars, more than $100,000 for every man, woman, and child in America, and we are still running $2 trillion annual deficits.
Meanwhile, the Biden-Harris Administration keeps spending.
I’m not running for Congress to be the lesser of two evils - I’m running because I want to leave my children an America worth defending. I’m going to Washington to restore our commitment to America, and the values that took us from a fragile new republic to the greatest nation on earth.
I’ll stand firm against giving your tax dollars to Chinese corporations, just like I did in Lansing. I’ll vote to make our communities safe, secure the border, get fentanyl off our streets, and get government out of the way of our job-creators.
We will make our country work again, for every family, and along the way, I promise to be a congressman you can be proud of.
On November 5th, I hope you will vote for to represent the people of Michigan’s 7th District.
